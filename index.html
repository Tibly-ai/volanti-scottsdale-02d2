At Volanti Scottsdale, every table has a view

Perched above the runway at Scottsdale Airport, Volanti is a scratch kitchen that prioritizes fresh, seasonal offerings — from our cult-classic Volanti Burger and the Hundred Dollar Hamburger to weekend brunch with the McDowell Mountains on the horizon. Watch private jets and helicopters come and go from the patio, settle in at the bar for a Saguaro or an Espresso Martini, or let our in-house sommelier curate a wine locker of your own. Dining, cocktails and runway views — in true Scottsdale style.

Happy hour on the runway

Weekdays from 3 – 6 PM
At the bar and on the patio

Grab a seat at the bar or out on the patio and settle in for a specialty cocktail, a house highball, a local craft beer or a glass of house wine as the evening flights roll in. Graze through Short Rib Tacos, Volanti Meatballs, Tiny Tuna tartare, BBQ Pork Sliders and the Take Off Trio with house focaccia — and toast the sunset with half off all champagne bottles and select red and white wines.
