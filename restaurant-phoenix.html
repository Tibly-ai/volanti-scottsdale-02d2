Restaurant in Phoenix

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 22, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale delivers a runway-side dining experience overlooking the Scottsdale Airport and the McDowell Mountains, serving guests across the Phoenix metropolitan area. Located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, Volanti features a scratch kitchen, craft cocktails, weekday happy hour, weekend brunch, and exclusive helicopter dining packages with no boarding pass or security checkpoint required.

A Scratch Kitchen Dining Destination for Phoenix Readers

For diners in Phoenix seeking an elevated culinary experience beyond traditional neighborhood spots, Volanti Scottsdale brings together chef-driven menus and unobstructed aviation views. Every table offers a vantage point over the active Scottsdale Airport runway, providing a distinctive backdrop for business lunches, sunset dinners, and weekend gatherings.

The culinary team emphasizes fresh, seasonal flavors prepared daily. From shareable plates at the bar to full multi-course dinners, the scratch kitchen approaches classic dining with modern technique. Located just a short drive from central Phoenix, the venue combines the energy of private aviation with an approachable, welcoming dining room and outdoor patio.

Exploring the Volanti Menu Options

Whether visiting for a mid-week business lunch or a late-night weekend celebration, the Volanti dining menus cater to a wide range of tastes and dining preferences.

Lunch and Daytime Dining

Served Tuesday through Friday, the lunch menu features fresh starter plates, seasonal salads, and satisfying entrees designed for efficient business meals or relaxed afternoon dining. Popular choices include:

  • Focaccia Bread: Served with fresh serrano pesto.
  • Rosemary Garlic Hummus: Topped with feta cheese, farmer's market vegetables, and grilled flatbread.
  • Take Off Trio: Featuring roasted tomato, serrano pesto, goat cheese, and house focaccia.
  • Cardini Salad: Romaine, house-made caesar dressing, parmesan, and garlic crumble.

Dinner Service Above the Runway

As night falls, the runway lights take center stage. The dinner selection incorporates prime steaks, fresh seafood, and chef-driven house specialties:

  • Volanti Meatballs: House blend of veal, ground sirloin, and pork sausage topped with marinara and parmesan.
  • Fresh Burrata: Accompanied by serrano pesto, roasted tomato, arugula, balsamic glaze, and pistachio.
  • Bubbling Shrimp Scampi: Roasted tomato, parmesan, garlic, herbs, butter, and bread crumbs served with focaccia.
  • Wild Mushroom Pasta: Tagliatelle pasta tossed in chipotle cream sauce, tomato, avocado, and queso fresco.

Weekday Happy Hour

Hosted Monday through Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar, happy hour brings together discounted drinks and shareable bites as evening jets taxi past. Guests enjoy specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, house wines, and half-off all champagne bottles alongside items like BBQ Pork Sliders, Tiny Tuna Tartare, and Short Rib Tacos.

Unique Aviation Experiences & Private Dining

Volanti extends beyond traditional restaurant service by integrating private aviation culture into its core hospitality offerings.

Helicopter Tour & Dining Packages

For those celebrating a special occasion in Phoenix, the Helicopter Tour Experience couples a customized private helicopter flight over the Valley with a three-course meal at the restaurant. Each reservation includes a $150 credit toward food and beverages from the main menu, creating an unforgettable date night or group flight.

On-Site Wine Lockers

Wine enthusiasts can apply for the Volanti Wine Locker program. Members work directly with the in-house sommelier to curate a private cellar stored on-site. Membership features exclusive perks, including priority patio reservations, invitations to private wine dinners, access to rare vintages, and discounts on event space buyouts.

Private Events and Jet Catering

Volanti manages custom event venues at the Scottsdale Airport, capable of hosting groups ranging from intimate patio gatherings of 15 to large-scale hangar galas for up to 2,000 guests. Additionally, Volanti Jet Catering delivers the full restaurant menu directly to private aircraft departing the airport.

Visitor Logistics for Phoenix Guests

Planning a visit to Volanti Scottsdale from Phoenix is straightforward and stress-free:

  • Location: 15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, Arizona 85260.
  • Access: Situated on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center overlooking the runway. No boarding pass, TSA screening, or airport security checkpoint is required to enter.
  • Parking: Free covered parking is available directly across from the main entrance.
  • Reservations: Table bookings can be made online for lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch. Walk-ins are always welcomed at the bar, dining room, and patio. Parties of 8 or more should call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a plane ticket or security pass to eat at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti Scottsdale is an open-to-the-public restaurant located inside the Aviation Business Center. You do not need a boarding pass, ticket, or security clearance to enter the restaurant, bar, or patio.

How do I make a reservation for a large group?

For general dining, reservations can be made online. If you are dining with a party of 8 or more guests, please call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426 so the team can arrange appropriate seating.

What are the hours for Happy Hour at Volanti?

Happy hour runs Monday through Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM. It features small bites, craft beer, house wine, specialty cocktails, and half-off bottles of champagne on the patio and at the bar.

Where do guests park when visiting from Phoenix?

Convenient covered parking is located directly across the street from the Aviation Business Center building at 15000 North Airport Drive.

What is included in the Helicopter Tour dining package?

The helicopter tour experience includes a customized private flight over the Valley for two (with larger helicopters available for up to five guests) paired with a $150 food and beverage credit to use toward lunch, brunch, or dinner at Volanti.
