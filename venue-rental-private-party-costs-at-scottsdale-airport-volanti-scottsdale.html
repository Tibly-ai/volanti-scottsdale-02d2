Factors That Determine Private Dining & Event Pricing

Calculating the precise budget for an event or private corporate menu at a luxury airport venue involves evaluating several operational factors:

  1. Guest Count & Space Allocation: Smaller groups up to 15 guests fit naturally on the outdoor patio, while larger parties transition into the indoor Thunderbird & Stearman rooms (up to 200) or expansive executive hangars (up to 2,000).
  2. Time of Day & Day of Week: Midday corporate lunches, weekday happy hours (3:00 PM – 6:00 PM), and full evening buyout dinners carry distinct food and beverage minimums based on peak service hours.
  3. Menu Selections: Scratch kitchen offerings feature prime steaks, fresh seafood, house-made focaccia, and specialized plated or family-style course packages tailored to corporate guests.
  4. Beverage & Bar Packages: Menu pricing adjusts based on craft cocktail additions, local craft beer selections, house highballs, sommelier-curated wine lists, and half-off champagne bottle tiers during happy hour windows.
  5. Aviation & Experiential Add-Ons: Corporate events can be paired with exclusive flight experiences, such as a customized helicopter tour for two or custom flight packages using multiple helicopters and flight plans through our helicopter flight experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does luxury corporate event catering cost near the Scottsdale airport?

Event and catering pricing at Volanti Scottsdale depends on your group size, preferred space, and food and beverage choices. What are the capacity limits for private events at Volanti Scottsdale?

Our venue options accommodate groups of varying sizes: the outdoor patio hosts up to 15 guests for casual gatherings; the indoor Thunderbird & Stearman rooms host up to 200 guests for seated dinners and corporate functions; and our executive hangars offer over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop space for up to 2,000 attendees.

Can Volanti cater directly to private aircraft on the runway?

Yes. Volanti Jet Catering brings our complete scratch menu directly to your aircraft at Scottsdale Airport, offering corporate flight crews and private aviation travelers fresh, chef-driven meals tailored for in-flight dining.
