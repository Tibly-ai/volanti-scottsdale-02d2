Volanti Scottsdale vs Talavera: How to Choose

Volanti Scottsdale offers a scratch kitchen, craft cocktails, and premier runway-side dining overlooking the Scottsdale Airport and the McDowell Mountains in Scottsdale, Arizona. Located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive, Volanti serves lunch, dinner, weekend brunch, and weekday happy hour with no boarding pass or security checkpoint required.

Choosing the right destination for a upscale meal, patio gathering, or private event in the Scottsdale and Phoenix market comes down to evaluating atmosphere, venue features, culinary focus, and logistical accessibility. While Talavera is another dining provider buyers consider in the Valley, reviewing specific service parameters—such as scratch-kitchen dining, direct runway views, unique aerial experiences, and flexible event spaces—helps ensure your selection aligns with your group's exact needs.

Key Factors to Evaluate When Selecting a Restaurant in Scottsdale

When evaluating fine dining and event venues across Scottsdale and Phoenix, reviewing several operational and atmospheric details ensures a seamless experience for your party:

  • Location and Accessibility: Check parking convenience, floor levels, and whether access requires special passes or security clearances. Volanti Scottsdale is situated on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with covered parking right across from the building, requiring zero boarding passes or security checkpoints.
  • Atmosphere and Views: Determine whether the venue offers standard patio seating or unique architectural vantage points. Volanti features clear sightlines of private jets taxiing alongside sweeping views of the McDowell Mountains.
  • Culinary Approach: Inquire whether the menu relies on a scratch kitchen with fresh, seasonal ingredients and versatile dining options, ranging from small plates to prime steaks and fresh seafood.
  • Specialty Offerings and Experiences: Look for signature dining experiences, such as pairing a multi-course meal with a private helicopter flight over the Valley or maintaining an on-site wine locker.
  • Private Event Adaptability: Ensure the venue provides scalable event options, whether hosting an intimate patio party for 15, a seated dinner for 200, or a massive hangar event for up to 2,000 guests.

The Volanti Dining & Hospitality Experience

At Volanti Scottsdale, every table features a view of the Scottsdale Airport runway and the surrounding mountain landscape. The kitchen operates as a scratch kitchen, serving chef-driven lunch, dinner, weekend brunch, and weekday happy hour menus.

Scratch Kitchen & Menu Highlights

The dining menu offers a broad range of shared plates, fresh salads, prime cuts, and seafood. Guests can enjoy signature starters like the Volanti Meatballs (a house blend of veal, ground sirloin, and pork sausage), Fresh Burrata, or the Take Off Trio with house focaccia. Entrees feature options such as the Wild Mushroom Pasta, Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken, and prime steaks. For lunch or happy hour, guest favorites include Short Rib Tacos, BBQ Pork Sliders, and Tiny Tuna Tartare.

Weekday Happy Hour

Hosted weekdays from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar, happy hour features specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, house wine, and half-off all champagne bottles alongside select food specials while aircraft taxi past. You can explore full selections on our menus page.

Experiential Dining: Helicopter Tours & Wine Lockers

For diners seeking unique outings in Scottsdale, Volanti integrates its airport location directly into its hospitality packages:

  • Helicopter Tour & Dinner Experience: Volanti offers a custom date-night flight package for two (with larger aircraft available for up to five guests, or custom multi-helicopter packages for larger groups). The experience includes a flight around the Valley paired with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner at the restaurant, along with a $150 food and beverage credit towards your meal. Learn more or book directly on our helicopter tour page.
  • Private Wine Locker Program: Wine enthusiasts can store a personalized collection on-site curated with the assistance of Volanti's in-house sommelier. Locker members receive exclusive perks, including priority patio reservations, first access to wine dinners, discounts on venue rentals, and access to limited-release bottles. Detailed information is available on our wine lockers page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a ticket or security pass to eat at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. It is completely open to the public, and no boarding pass, security checkpoint, or flight ticket is required.

What are the parking options at Volanti Scottsdale?

Covered parking is located directly across from the Aviation Business Center building at 15000 North Airport Drive.

What is included in the Helicopter Tour package?

The Helicopter Tour package includes a customized flight around the Valley for two (or up to five in larger aircraft) along with a $150 credit towards food and beverages for a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner at Volanti.

What are the weekday Happy Hour hours and deals?

Happy Hour runs Monday through Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM at the bar and on the patio. It features specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, house wine, small bites, and half-off champagne bottles.

How do I make a reservation for large groups?

Walk-ins are always welcome at Volanti Scottsdale. Guests can reserve tables online, but parties of 8 or more are asked to call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426 to arrange seating.
