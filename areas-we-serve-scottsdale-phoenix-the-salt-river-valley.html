Areas We Serve: Scottsdale, Phoenix & the Salt River Valley

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 24, 2026

slug: areas-we-serve

Volanti Scottsdale serves guests, travelers, and event hosts from Scottsdale, Phoenix, and across Arizona from our location at the Scottsdale Airport. Situated on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive, Volanti provides scratch-kitchen dining, craft cocktails, executive hangar events, private wine lockers, helicopter tour packages, and private jet catering for the entire Phoenix metropolitan area.

Airport Dining and Event Services for Scottsdale, Phoenix, and Arizona

Volanti Scottsdale offers culinary and aviation-focused experiences for visitors from Scottsdale, Phoenix, and the broader Arizona region. Located directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport, our venue provides panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains alongside scratch-made cuisine and craft beverages. Guests access our main dining room and patio on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with no boarding pass or security checkpoint required. Covered parking is located directly across from the building.

Serving Scottsdale

Located in North Scottsdale, Volanti serves local residents, corporate groups, and travelers seeking scratch-kitchen meals or private gathering spaces. Guests visit us for lunch, dinner, weekend brunch, and weekday happy hour on the patio. Our venues host events ranging from small patio birthday gatherings for 15 guests to dinner celebrations for 200 in our Thunderbird & Stearman rooms.

Serving Phoenix

Volanti welcomes diners and event planners from neighboring Phoenix who are searching for runway views and specialized aviation hospitality. Phoenix businesses and private clients utilize Volanti Jet Catering to bring full-service meals directly to their aircraft at Scottsdale Airport, or book large-scale corporate galas and fundraisers for up to 2,000 guests in our executive hangars.

Serving broader Arizona and regional travelers

Visitors arriving in Arizona via private or general aviation use Volanti as their primary dining and event destination at Scottsdale Airport. From paired helicopter dining flights over the Valley to exclusive membership in our private wine locker program, Volanti delivers upscale hospitality tailored to aviation enthusiasts and regional guests.

Experiential Offerings at Volanti Scottsdale

Every dining experience and event at Volanti comes with a direct runway view. Whether you are joining us for a casual weekday meal or planning a multi-thousand-guest event, our offerings bridge chef-driven cuisine with aviation.

Scratch Kitchen & Craft Cocktails

Our kitchen prepares chef-driven scratch menus featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, and signature appetizers. Popular dishes across our lunch, dinner, and brunch menus include Short Rib Tacos, Volanti Meatballs, Tiny Tuna Tartare, BBQ Pork Sliders, and the Take Off Trio served with house-made focaccia. Our beverage program features specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, and wine selected by our in-house sommelier.

Weekday Happy Hour on the Runway

We host happy hour on our patio and bar weekdays from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Guests can enjoy small bites and drinks as evening flights taxi past, along with half-off all champagne bottles and select wine options.

Helicopter Tour & Dining Packages

Guests looking for flight experiences paired with fine dining can book our Helicopter Tour package. This experience pairs a private flight over the Valley for two (with options for up to five guests) with a $150 food and beverage credit toward a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner at Volanti.

Private Wine Lockers

Our wine locker program allows members to store a sommelier-curated selection on-site for exclusive use during visits. Wine locker members receive VIP benefits, including early access to new offerings, priority patio reservations, invitations to wine dinners, and venue discounts.

Hangar Events and Private Celebrations

Volanti Hangar Events operates over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop event space at Scottsdale Airport. Our dedicated events team manages private celebrations, weddings, corporate dinners, and large fundraisers.

Volanti Jet Catering

Volanti Jet Catering delivers our chef-driven scratch menu directly to aircraft stationed at Scottsdale Airport, ensuring travelers receive high-quality meals on their flights.

How to Visit Volanti Scottsdale

Volanti is open to the public Tuesday through Sunday. We welcome walk-ins, though reservations are recommended for peak dining hours and small parties.

  • Address: 15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, Arizona 85260 (Second floor of the Aviation Business Center).
  • Parking: Covered parking is available right across from the main entrance.
  • Access: No boarding pass, flight ticket, or security checkpoint is required to enter the building or restaurant.
  • Large Parties: For parties of 8 or more guests, please call our reservations team at 480.657.2426.

Operating Hours

  • Lunch: Tuesday – Friday, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Dinner: Tuesday – Thursday, 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM; Friday & Saturday, 4:30 PM – 10:00 PM
  • Brunch: Saturday & Sunday, 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Happy Hour: Weekdays (Monday – Friday), 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a flight ticket or security pass to eat at Volanti?

No. Volanti is fully open to the general public. We are located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport, and no boarding pass, security checkpoint, or flight reservation is required to enter.

What cities and areas does Volanti Scottsdale serve?

Volanti serves guests across Scottsdale, Phoenix, and the greater Arizona region. We welcome local diners, airport visitors, corporate clients hosting events, and aircraft passengers requiring jet catering at Scottsdale Airport.

How do I make a reservation for a large group?

For parties of 8 or more, please call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426 so our team can accommodate your group. For smaller groups, you can make a reservation online through our website or visit as a walk-in.

Where do guests park when visiting Volanti?

Covered parking is located directly across from the Aviation Business Center building at 15000 North Airport Drive, providing convenient access to the restaurant elevator and stairs.

What event venues are available at the airport?

We offer the runway patio for groups around 15, the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for dinner celebrations up to 200 guests, and over 30,000 square feet of executive hangar, runway, and rooftop event space capable of hosting events for up to 2,000 guests. Learn more on our experiences page.

Does Volanti offer food catering for private jets?

Yes. Volanti Jet Catering brings our scratch-kitchen menu directly to private aircraft parked at Scottsdale Airport.
