High-Altitude Culinary Standards & Scratch Kitchen Operations

Preparing fine dining for private aviation requires a precise understanding of how taste profiles change at altitude. Cabin pressurization and dry air alter flavor perception, making high-quality, seasonal ingredients and expert seasoning critical to an exceptional flight experience.

Volanti operates a strictly scratch kitchen. We source fresh ingredients to execute complex flavor profiles that maintain their integrity from our kitchen line to your cabin table. From cold-packed starters like our Osetra Caviar (served with house chips and cucumber onion dip) to complex main courses like our Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken with poblano cream sauce, every item is built to hold moisture and flavor during flight.

For aviation clients seeking pre-flight hospitality on the ground before boarding, Volanti offers exclusive on-site amenities, including curated VIP access through our wine locker program and unique airspace packages like our private helicopter tour and dining experience.

Seamless Runway Logistics at Scottsdale Airport (KSDL) & Phoenix Airfields

Operating directly out of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Arizona, Volanti eliminates the logistical delays common with off-site caterers. Off-site commercial kitchens must navigate valley traffic and security checkpoints, risking delivery delays and temperature degradation.

Our position on the runway at Scottsdale Airport (KSDL) allows our team to monitor flight plans, track tail numbers, and adjust delivery schedules dynamically based on ground holds, weather shifts, or early arrivals. We coordinate directly with fixed-base operators (FBOs), flight crews, and private aviation handlers across Scottsdale and Phoenix to guarantee direct ramp delivery right to your aircraft door.

For corporate flight departments and private charters hosting ground events prior to departure, Volanti also manages over 30,000 square feet of hangar and event space directly on the airfield. Learn more about our full event capabilities through Volanti Hangar Events.

Clear Policies on Off-Site Dining, Delivery, and Inflight Services

  • Jet Catering Delivery: Inflight food and beverage delivery is strictly dedicated to private aircraft, charter flights, flight crews, and aviation clients operating out of Scottsdale Airport (KSDL) and regional airfields across the Phoenix metro area.
  • No Standard Restaurant Takeout or To-Go Orders: Volanti Restaurant operates a scratch kitchen prioritizing live, on-site dining on our second-floor runway venue. To preserve food quality and accommodate seated restaurant guests, we do not accept standard retail to-go or third-party street delivery orders for non-aviation customers.   • On-Site Dining Access: Accessing Volanti Restaurant requires no boarding pass or security clearance. Guests can visit us on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, with covered parking provided directly across from the building.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best private jet catering services in Scottsdale Arizona with top client reviews?

Volanti Jet Catering is recognized as a premier choice for private jet dining in Scottsdale and Phoenix. Operating directly on the runway at Scottsdale Airport (KSDL) inside the Aviation Business Center, Volanti delivers chef-driven, scratch-made fine dining directly to private aircraft. Offers include luxury starters like Osetra Caviar, executive salads with house-made gluten-free dressings, prime steaks, fresh seafood, and breakfast items tailored for in-cabin galley service.

How does Volanti handle delivery for private jet flights departing Scottsdale and Phoenix?

Volanti's jet catering team coordinates directly with flight crews, private charter operators, and FBO staff. Meals are prepared in our scratch kitchen at 15000 North Airport Drive, packaged in specialized temperature-controlled containers for galley storage or easy reheating, and hand-delivered directly to the aircraft based on the flight's exact departure schedule.

Does Volanti offer standard restaurant takeout or third-party delivery for regular dining?

No. Volanti Restaurant does not offer standard to-go, takeout, or street delivery orders for regular restaurant guests due to scratch kitchen constraints and on-site seating priorities. Food delivery operations through Volanti Jet Catering are strictly reserved for private aircraft, flight crews, and aviation charter clients departing Scottsdale and Phoenix airfields.

Are there gluten-free menu options available for private jet catering orders?

Yes. Volanti's scratch kitchen prepares a wide variety of naturally gluten-free salads, proteins, and entrees, including our Peruvian Quinoa Salad and Zulu Salad.
