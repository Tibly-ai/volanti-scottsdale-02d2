Immediate Access and Walk-In Policies

Visitors arriving without an advance reservation are accommodated on a first-come, first-served basis. Walk-ins are always welcome during standard operational hours. Because the restaurant sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, no security checkpoint or TSA boarding pass is required to enter the restaurant, bar, or patio dining spaces. Covered parking is situated directly across from the main building entrance.

If a specific dining time is not visible in the online reservation system, or if you are managing an urgent, same-day booking for a party of eight or more guests, contact the front of house staff directly by phone at 480.657.2426.

Urgent Jet Catering Requests

For flight crews, corporate aviation dispatchers, and private jet passengers needing immediate culinary service at Scottsdale Airport or surrounding Valley airports, Volanti Jet Catering provides fresh kitchen preparation designed to travel directly aboard aircraft.

The chef-driven scratch kitchen prepares items to hold temperature and transport smoothly. To coordinate an urgent order or fulfill tight aircraft departure deadlines:

  1. Review the available culinary items, small bites, and full entrees on our published dining menus.
  2. Contact the culinary team directly by calling 480.657.2426 to state your required delivery or pickup time and aircraft tail number.
  3. Confirm any specific packaging or delivery requirements directly with the kitchen staff.

Same-Day Large Group and Event Coordination

Organizing a large gathering, corporate dinner, or impromptu celebration on short notice requires clear venue limits and immediate channel routing. Volanti manages on-site and hangar-based logistics at Scottsdale Airport, serving guests across Scottsdale, Phoenix, and the broader Arizona area.

Depending on the scale of your urgent gathering, the venue offers three primary configurations:

  • Patio Gatherings: Designed for intimate groups of up to 15 guests looking for open-air seating overlooking the runway during lunch, dinner, or weekday happy hour.
  • Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms: Indoor private dining configurations capable of hosting dinner celebrations and formal sit-down events for up to 200 guests.
  • Executive Hangars: Massive event structures offering over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop event space accommodating major galas, product reveals, and large fundraisers for up to 2,000 guests.

For immediate group assistance or same-day space availability, call 480.657.2426 to speak directly with the dedicated event management team.

Experience Bookings and Scheduling Adjustments

For guests participating in specialized experiences such as the Helicopter Tour and dining package, arrival times are strictly managed to keep flight paths on schedule. Guests must arrive at least 15 minutes prior to their scheduled flight reservation time to check in at the restaurant before proceeding down to the private runway.

Because flight operations are tied to pilot scheduling, aircraft maintenance, and air traffic control, the following operational conditions apply to urgent timing adjustments:

  • Inclement Weather: Flight times remain subject to change or delay due to sudden weather shifts or unexpected helicopter availability.
  • Cancellation Policy: Due to booking schedules, refunds are not available for helicopter packages.
  • Rescheduling: Requests to move a flight time due to late arrival or sudden schedule changes are handled at the sole discretion of Volanti management and the aircraft pilots, subject to strictly verified availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you open on weekends?

Yes. Volanti is open on Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM (serving Brunch from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM and Dinner from 4:30 PM to 10:00 PM) and on Sundays from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM (serving Weekend Brunch).

Do I need a security pass or ticket to enter the restaurant?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the public Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. No TSA checkpoint, boarding pass, or security clearance is required to enter the building or eat at the restaurant.

What should I do if online reservations show no available tables?

If you do not see your preferred dining time online, walk-ins are always welcomed in the dining room, at the bar, and on the patio. For parties of eight or more, call 480.657.2426 so the team can review table arrangements directly.

Do you offer same-day jet catering for flights at Scottsdale Airport?

Yes. Volanti Jet Catering prepares scratch-kitchen meals for private aircraft departing from Scottsdale Airport and regional Valley airports. Call 480.657.2426 to coordinate food preparation and delivery times with the kitchen.

What happens if bad weather impacts a scheduled helicopter tour?

Helicopter flight schedules are subject to change due to inclement weather or helicopter availability. While cash or credit refunds are not provided, rescheduling is handled based on pilot availability and management discretion.
