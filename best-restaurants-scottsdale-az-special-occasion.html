What are the best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 9, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale is a top choice for special occasion dining, romantic date nights, and landmark celebrations in Scottsdale, Arizona. Located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport, Volanti offers a scratch kitchen featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, and craft cocktails set against floor-to-ceiling views of the active runway and the McDowell Mountains.

Upscale Fine Dining Above the Runway at Scottsdale Airport

Finding the ideal venue for an anniversary, milestone birthday, or romantic date night in the Phoenix and Scottsdale area requires an atmosphere that combines culinary excellence with a distinctive setting. Volanti Scottsdale delivers an elevated dining experience directly overlooking the active airport runway without requiring a boarding pass or security checkpoint.

Guests can choose between scratch-kitchen dining in the main dining room, craft cocktails during weekday happy hour on the patio, or custom experiential packages that pair culinary offerings with private aviation. Volanti pairs fine dining with private aviation experiences, creating a unique alternative to traditional Valley steakhouses. Covered parking is available right across from the building for convenient guest access.

What are the best upscale restaurants in North Scottsdale?

When evaluating upscale dining in North Scottsdale, Volanti stands out by combining chef-driven scratch cooking with panoramic views of corporate jets and the McDowell Mountains. Operating from the Aviation Business Center, Volanti provides a fresh perspective on Arizona fine dining.

The kitchen focuses on high-quality ingredients across its menus:

  • Lunch & Dinner: Featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, hand-crafted pastas, fresh burrata, tuna tartare, and house specialties.
  • Craft Cocktails & Wine Selection: A curated list of house cocktails, spritzes, zero-proof options, and an extensive wine selection managed alongside our wine locker program.
  • Weekend Brunch: Served every Saturday and Sunday, featuring elevated brunch favorites with runway views.
  • Weekday Happy Hour: Hosted Monday through Friday from 3 to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar, featuring specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, small plates, and half-off champagne bottles.

Whether hosting an intimate dinner in the Stearman or Thunderbird rooms or enjoying sunset drinks on the outdoor patio, Volanti provides a refined setting tailored for memorable gatherings.

Best fine dining spots in Scottsdale for watching the sunset over the mountains

Sunset dining in the Valley is defined by mountain backdrops and open skies. Volanti offer clear views of the sun dipping behind the McDowell Mountains as evening flights taxi past.

Guests looking to elevate their sunset experience can combine dining with private aviation:

Three-Course Helicopter Tour & Date Night Packages

For couples seeking a date night experience, Volanti pairs fine dining with an aerial flight around the Valley:

  1. Flight & Dining Integration: Guests check in at the restaurant 15 minutes prior to their scheduled flight time, proceed to the private runway for a scenic flight over Phoenix and Scottsdale, and return to their table for a meal.
  2. Flexible Dining Pairings: The helicopter tour package can be paired with a three-course lunch, weekend brunch, or dinner.
  3. Custom Group Packages: While standard flights seat two passengers, larger helicopters are available for groups up to five, and custom multi-helicopter flight plans can be arranged for larger parties.

Private Events, Catering, and Hangar Celebrations

For larger landmark events, Volanti extends its culinary services beyond the main restaurant floor through dedicated event spaces and private aviation catering:

  • Executive Hangar Events: Volanti manages over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop event space at Scottsdale Airport. The dedicated events team handles gatherings ranging from patio parties of 15 guests to full hangar galas, fundraisers, and weddings for up to 2,000 attendees.
  • Volanti Jet Catering: The restaurant's culinary team provides specialized jet catering services, delivering scratch-prepared meals directly to private aircraft departing Scottsdale Airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion?

Volanti Scottsdale is consistently recognized as one of the premier special occasion restaurants in the Phoenix and Scottsdale area. It combines a scratch kitchen offering prime steaks and fresh seafood with clear views of the Scottsdale Airport runway and McDowell Mountains. Experiential add-ons like private helicopter tour packages and wine lockers make it ideal for anniversaries, birthdays, and date nights.

What are the best upscale restaurants in North Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale offers fine dining in North Scottsdale, located at 15000 North Airport Drive. Situated above the runway, Volanti provides chef-driven lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch menus, an extensive wine program, weekday happy hour specials, and private event rooms.

What are the best fine dining spots in Scottsdale for watching the sunset over the mountains?

Volanti's second-floor dining room and outdoor patio provide unobstructed views of the sunset over the McDowell Mountains while private jets taxi along the runway. Guests can enjoy craft cocktails, wine, and scratch-made entrees during sunset hours, or book a helicopter dining experience to view the sunset from the air before dinner.

Do I need a boarding pass or airport security clearance to eat at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti is located inside the public Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. No boarding pass or airport security screening is required to visit the restaurant, bar, or patio. Covered parking is located directly across from the building entrance.

How do helicopter tour and dinner packages work at Volanti?

Guests arrive and check in at Volanti Scottsdale 15 minutes prior to their scheduled flight time. After checking in, guests head down to the private runway for a flight around the Phoenix and Scottsdale area, returning to the restaurant to enjoy a three-course meal paired with a food and beverage credit.

How can I make a reservation for a large party or special event?

Walk-ins are welcome at Volanti. Reservations for lunch, dinner, and brunch can be booked directly online through our experiences and reservations page. For parties of 8 or more, or to inquire about booking private event rooms and executive hangars, guests can call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426.
