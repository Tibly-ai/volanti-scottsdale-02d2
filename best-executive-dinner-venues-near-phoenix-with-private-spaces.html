Best Executive Dinner Venues Near Phoenix with Private Spaces

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 22, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale offers premier executive dining and business lunches overlooking the runway at Scottsdale Airport, just minutes from downtown Phoenix. Located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with dedicated private spaces—including the Thunderbird and Stearman rooms—Volanti combines scratch-kitchen cuisine, craft cocktails, and mountain views with seamless access, free covered parking, and no TSA or security checkpoints required.

Executive Dining & Business Lunch at Scottsdale Airport

Finding a executive dinner venue in the Phoenix metropolitan area requires balancing convenience, privacy, high-caliber cuisine, and an impressive atmosphere. Volanti Scottsdale delivers an elevated dining experience tailored specifically for corporate gatherings, executive board dinners, client meetings, and high-impact business lunches.

Perched directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport, Volanti provides an energetic backdrop of taxiing private jets and panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains. Whether hosting an intimate strategy lunch for eight or a major corporate dinner for 200 guests, our kitchen serves fresh, seasonal scratch dishes, prime steaks, fresh seafood, and signature cocktails designed to elevate any business meeting.

For local Phoenix executives, visiting board members, and business travelers arriving via private aviation, Volanti eliminates the typical logistical friction of fine dining. Guests arrive directly at 15000 North Airport Drive, park in the covered garage located directly across from the building, and take the elevator straight to the second floor without dealing with airport security or boarding passes.

Private Dining Spaces & Corporate Event Venues

Volanti features flexible private and semi-private venue spaces designed to accommodate business groups of varying sizes, from confidential pitch meetings to large-scale company celebrations.

The Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms

Designed specifically for private seated events, corporate presentations, and formal executive dinners, the Thunderbird and Stearman rooms comfortably accommodate up to 200 guests. These dedicated rooms combine privacy with sweeping glass views of the Scottsdale Airport runway and the McDowell Mountains, creating a quiet environment for presentations, awards dinners, and multi-course meals.

Patio Dining

For casual executive lunches or sunset happy hour meetings, Volanti’s outdoor patio seats small-to-medium groups—such as a birthday or deal-closing celebration for 15—directly alongside the runway. Enjoy fresh air, local craft beers, and half-off champagne bottles during weekday happy hour while reviewing business plans.

Executive Hangars

For enterprise-level business events, product launches, corporate galas, and major fundraisers, Volanti provides access to over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop event space. The executive hangars cater to massive corporate gatherings of up to 2,000 guests beneath aircraft wings.

Scratch Kitchen Menus for Business Meals

Corporate meetings require predictable timing, exceptional flavor, and menu diversity to accommodate all guests. Volanti’s chef-driven scratch kitchen operates daily across lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch service.

  • Business Lunch (Tuesday – Friday, 11 AM – 3 PM): Perfect for quick, impressive midday meetings. Start with shareables like Rosemary Garlic Hummus with grilled flatbread, Volanti Meatballs, or the Take Off Trio with house focaccia. For main courses, choose from healthy salads like the Peruvian Quinoa or Cardini Caesar (with optional grilled chicken, steak, salmon, or king prawns), or savory entrees like Short Rib Tacos and BBQ Pork Sliders.
  • Executive Dinner (Tuesday – Thursday 4:30 – 9 PM, Friday & Saturday 4:30 – 10 PM): Sophisticated evening dining featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house specialties. Share dishes such as Bubbling Shrimp Scampi or Ahi Tuna Bowl, followed by entrees like Wild Mushroom Pasta in chipotle cream sauce, Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken with chive mashed potatoes, or Osetra Caviar service.
  • Craft Cocktails & Wine Program: Complete your dinner meeting with curated wine options or signature cocktails like the Hugo Spritz or Summer Solstice. Volanti also maintains an exclusive Wine Locker program curated by our in-house sommelier for frequent executive guests.

Why Phoenix Executives Choose Volanti

  • No Security Checkpoint Required: Located inside the Aviation Business Center, guests walk straight into the second-floor restaurant without going through airport security.
  • Convenient Covered Parking: Dedicated covered parking is located directly across from the main entrance, ensuring smooth arrivals and departures for busy professionals.
  • Proximity to Phoenix & Scottsdale Tech Corridors: Situated right on Airport Drive, Volanti is central to North Scottsdale corporate parks and easily accessible from Greater Phoenix highways.
  • In-Flight Jet Catering: For executives flying directly out of Scottsdale Airport, Volanti Jet Catering delivers the full restaurant menu directly to your aircraft before takeoff.

Key Takeaways

  • Location: Volanti is on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive, overlooking the Scottsdale Airport runway.
  • Ease of Access: Free covered parking across from the building; no boarding pass or security checkpoint required to enter.
  • Private Room Capacity: The Thunderbird and Stearman rooms seat up to 200 guests for private executive dinners and meetings.
  • Large Event Space: Over 30,000 square feet of hangar and rooftop spaces host corporate events for up to 2,000 attendees.
  • Hours: Open for Lunch (Tue–Fri), Dinner (Tue–Sat), Weekend Brunch (Sat–Sun), and Weekday Happy Hour (3–6 PM).

Frequently Asked Questions

Best executive dinner venues near Phoenix with private spaces and easy parking?

How do I make a reservation for a corporate group or business lunch at Volanti?

Does Volanti offer catering for executive flights out of Scottsdale Airport?

Yes. Through Volanti Jet Catering, our kitchen delivers fresh meals, appetizers, and beverages directly to private aircraft on the runway for seamless dining in the air.
