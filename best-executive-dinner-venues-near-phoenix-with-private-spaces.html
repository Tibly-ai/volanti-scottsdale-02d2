Volanti Scottsdale provides a corporate dining experience tailored specifically for board dinners, confidential pitch meetings, client entertainment, and high-impact executive gatherings in North Scottsdale.

The Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms

Designed specifically for formal executive dinners, corporate slide presentations, and board meetings, the Thunderbird and Stearman rooms comfortably seat up to 200 guests. These dedicated private spaces provide floor-to-ceiling glass framing the Scottsdale Airport runway, giving your board members and key clients an expansive view of private aviation against the backdrop of the McDowell Mountains. The private room environment ensures complete confidentiality for sensitive corporate discussions, executive recruitment dinners, and quarterly performance reviews.

Outdoor Patio Dining

For casual executive lunches, team milestone celebrations, or sunset happy hour meetings, Volanti’s outdoor patio offers open-air dining directly alongside the runway. Accommodating smaller groups of up to 15 guests, the patio allows corporate teams to toast closed deals while private jets taxi past.

Executive Hangars for Major Corporate Events

When executive dining expands into full-scale company galas, product launches, or charity fundraisers, Volanti Hangar Events provides access to over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop space. Holding up to 2,000 guests, these unique venues pair custom catering with aviation backdrops.
