Best Executive Dinner Venues Near Phoenix with Private Spaces

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 23, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale delivers the best executive dinner venues near Phoenix with private spaces, situated directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport. Executive Dining & Business Lunch at Scottsdale Airport Near Phoenix

Finding an executive dinner or business lunch venue near Phoenix requires balancing absolute privacy, high-caliber cuisine, seamless transport logistics, and an impressive physical atmosphere. Volanti Scottsdale delivers a specialized corporate dining environment built specifically for confidential pitch meetings, board dinners, client entertainment, and high-impact executive gatherings in the Phoenix metropolitan area.

Located just minutes from Phoenix on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, Volanti removes typical metropolitan dining friction. Guests visiting from Phoenix or arriving via private aviation park free in the covered garage located directly across from the building, enter the main lobby, and take the elevator straight to the second floor. There are no valet delays, street parking searches, or airport security barriers. For Phoenix business diners seeking impressive views and easy access, Volanti combines direct runway sights with scratch-kitchen culinary excellence.

Best Lunch Spots Near Phoenix Airport with Impressive Views for Business Meetings

When corporate leaders search for the best lunch spots near Phoenix airport with impressive views for business meetings, Volanti Scottsdale stands out by offering frictionless logistics alongside an elevated visual experience. Perched directly over the tarmac, every table looks out onto taxiing aircraft framed by the McDowell Mountains.

Business lunch is served Tuesday through Friday from 11 AM to 3 PM, offering chef-driven dishes suited for productive mid-day meetings:

  • Executive Starters: To Share items including Rosemary Garlic Hummus with feta and grilled flatbread, Take Off Trio with serrano pesto, roasted tomato, goat cheese, and house focaccia, or Fresh Burrata with serrano pesto, roasted tomato, and balsamic glaze.
  • Fresh Salads: Options such as the Zulu salad with field greens, napa cabbage, goat cheese, and lime vinaigrette, or the Peruvian salad with quinoa, arugula, golden raisins, and lemon. Protein add-ons include grilled chicken, steak, salmon, or king prawns.
  • Scratch Entrees: Prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house specials prepared daily by our scratch kitchen.

Because the venue sits outside TSA security checkpoints within the Aviation Business Center, guests land, park, and sit for a confidential business lunch without transit delays.

The Boardroom

Located inside the Aviation Business Center, the private Boardroom seats up to 10 people directly alongside the runway. Built specifically for executive strategy and pitch sessions, it comes equipped with an 8-foot whiteboard and a high-definition TV with HDMI inputs. Dedicated refreshment options include an in-room mini-fridge stocked with essentials, full-service coffee stations, or full in-meeting dining served directly from the kitchen.

The Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms

Designed for formal executive dinners, slide presentations, and large corporate gatherings, the Stearman and Thunderbird rooms seat up to 200 guests. Named in honor of the airport's origins as a WWII training facility, these rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the tarmac and McDowell Mountains. They can be booked individually or combined, with optional access to the Thunderbird patio. Included amenities feature 32 classroom-style meeting tables, 150 conference-style chairs, 100 white Mission chairs, a projector and screen, a complete A/V system with microphones, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a dedicated private entrance.

Executive Hangars

For large-scale events, galas, and major corporate announcements, Volanti controls exclusive access to hangars surrounding the airfield offering over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop space. These private spaces accommodate anywhere from 10-person private jet dinners up to 5,000-guest galas, complete with full stage, lighting, and custom aircraft backdrops.

Elevating Executive Dining: Cocktails, Experiences, and Membership

Beyond standard executive dinners, Volanti delivers specialized VIP programs and corporate experiences tailored for business entertaining:

  • Helicopter Tour & Dining: Pair a chef-curated three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner with a private helicopter flight around the Valley for an unforgettable executive client experience. Packages include a $150 food and beverage credit towards the full menu.
  • Wine Lockers Program: Executive diners can secure a personal cellar on-site. Managed alongside our sommelier, Volanti wine lockers give members personal bottle storage, priority patio reservations, exclusive access to wine dinners, and venue rental discounts.
  • Weekday Happy Hour: Take advantage of weekday happy hour from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio or at the bar, featuring specialty cocktails, local craft beer, small bites like Short Rib Tacos and Volanti Meatballs, and half-off all champagne bottles.

Scratch Kitchen Dining Options & Venue Policies

Volanti operates a scratch kitchen prioritizing fresh, seasonal ingredients. To ensure seamless planning for corporate organizers and Phoenix visitors, please review our key dining policies:

  • Dietary Accommodations: Scratch-made dressings (including sesame ginger, herb ranch, chipotle ranch, and lime vinaigrette) are 100% gluten-free. Menu items feature clear dietary indicators such as "V" for vegetarian (e.g., Focaccia Bread, Fresh Burrata, Wild Mushroom Pasta) and "GF" for gluten-free items (e.g., Peruvian Salad, Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken, Jumbo Shrimp Cocktail).
  • To-Go & Takeout Policy: Because our scratch kitchen prioritizes fresh, on-site service, Volanti does not offer standard to-go, takeout, or third-party delivery orders. Guests with limited time are invited to sit at the bar for prompt service, or arrange full in-flight catering via Volanti Jet Catering for departing private aircraft.
  • Atmosphere & Building Policies: Located in a City of Scottsdale facility, only certified service animals are permitted on the property and outdoor patio.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best lunch spots near Phoenix airport with impressive views for business meetings?

Volanti Scottsdale is one of the premier business lunch spots near Phoenix, located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. It offers direct views of private jets taking off against the McDowell Mountains, scratch-kitchen cuisine, free covered parking, and zero airport security checkpoints.

Do guests need a boarding pass or security clearance to dine at Volanti?

No. Volanti is open to the public on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center. You do not need a boarding pass, flight ticket, or TSA security clearance to access the restaurant or private dining spaces.

What private dining options are available for executive meetings near Phoenix?

Volanti offers several private spaces: an 10-person Boardroom equipped with an 8-foot whiteboard and HDMI-connected TV; the Thunderbird and Stearman rooms for up to 200 guests with built-in A/V capabilities; an outdoor patio for up to 15 guests; and expansive Executive Hangars accommodating up to 5,000 guests.

Where do executive dinner guests park at Volanti Scottsdale?

Guests park in the large, free covered parking garage directly across from the Aviation Business Center building entrance, providing effortless, covered elevator access to the second-floor dining rooms.

Does Volanti offer takeout or delivery for business lunches?

No. Volanti operates a scratch kitchen dedicated strictly to on-site dining and does not offer standard takeout, to-go, or third-party delivery options. For aviation clients, full menu catering is available via Volanti Jet Catering directly to departing aircraft.

Can we combine a business meeting with special dining experiences?

Yes. Corporate hosts can book a private flight package through the Volanti Helicopter Tour experience, reserve personal storage through the Volanti wine locker program, or work with the events team to host custom hangar gatherings.
