How Much Does a Private Business Lunch Cost at Top View Restaurants in Phoenix?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 6, 2026

A private business lunch or executive dining event at top view restaurants in Phoenix and Scottsdale varies based on your selected venue space, guest count, and menu selections, with guest minimums ranging from small team gatherings of 15 on the patio up to 200 guests in dedicated private dining rooms. Volanti Scottsdale, located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center overlooking the Scottsdale Airport runway, provides scratch-kitchen menus, craft cocktails, and mountain backdrops without requiring a boarding pass or security checkpoint.

Venue Spaces and Capacity for Phoenix Executive Business Lunches

Selecting the right space for a corporate gathering ensures your team enjoys privacy, full food and beverage service, and an engaging atmosphere. Corporate hosts planning executive lunches, board meetings, or regional business celebrations in the Phoenix metropolitan area can choose from distinct venue configurations above the runway:

  • Patio Gatherings: Built for team lunches, client wins, or mid-day team outings of up to 15 guests. Features runway-side outdoor patio seating with views of aircraft taxiing against the McDowell Mountains.
  • Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms: Designed for up to 200 guests requiring private indoor event spaces. Ideal for formal executive luncheons, company dinners, quarterly presentations, and enterprise board meetings directly overlooking the runway.
  • Executive Hangars: Expansive spaces encompassing over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop space. Built for large corporate fundraisers, regional summits, product launches, and major galas accommodating up to 2,000 attendees.

Key Operational and Culinary Cost Factors

When booking private dining spaces in the Phoenix and Scottsdale market, overall corporate event expenses depend on several specific event parameters rather than fixed off-the-shelf rates:

1. Guest Count and Venue Space Minimums

Smaller team lunches can be accommodated with reservations or patio gatherings for parties up to 15 guests. Larger corporate groups requiring full buyouts of dedicated indoor dining rooms or expansive hangar facilities are structured around total spatial requirements, guest minimums, and dedicated event staff support.

2. Time of Day and Dining Period

Booking an executive business event during the mid-day lunch service offers a distinct dining format compared to evening dinners or corporate happy hours. Mid-day events leverage scratch-driven lunch menus, allowing corporate planners to maintain efficient timelines while providing a high-end culinary experience.

3. Menu Selection and Customization

Costs are influenced by whether your gathering utilizes shared starters, plated lunch courses, specialized beverage pairings, or full custom menus crafted by the culinary team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private business lunch cost at top view restaurants in Phoenix?

Private business lunch pricing depends on group size, selected venue spaces, and customized menu formats. At Volanti Scottsdale, guest minimums begin with small gatherings of 15 guests on the patio, up to private indoor room buyouts for 200 guests in the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms, or large hangar events hosting up to 2,000. Final pricing reflects your selected food and beverage packages and space requirements.

Do guests need a boarding pass or TSA clearance to dine at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. Guests can walk right up to the restaurant without going through airport security or presenting a boarding pass.

Where do corporate lunch guests park at Scottsdale Airport?

Covered parking is available directly across the street from the Aviation Business Center building, providing easy access for executive guests and client attendees.

What private dining spaces are available for corporate meetings?

Volanti offers three primary spaces: an outdoor runway-side patio for gatherings up to 15 guests, the indoor Thunderbird & Stearman private rooms for up to 200 guests, and over 30,000 square feet of Executive Hangar space for enterprise events up to 2,000 guests.

Can business lunches be combined with custom experiences?

Yes. Corporate groups can combine lunch with a custom Valley helicopter tour, reserve space in our private wine locker program, or arrange corporate jet catering for private aviation flights departing Scottsdale Airport.
