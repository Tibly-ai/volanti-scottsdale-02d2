Wine Lockers

Do you have a passion for wine and spirits, love early access to the newest offerings, or simply enjoy having a personalized supply of wine at your fingertips every time you visit your favorite restaurant? Volanti’s wine locker program is the experience for you.

Our in-house sommelier will help you curate a wine locker selection that is stored on-site for your exclusive use. Reserve your locker today and enjoy your next glass with friends and family while overlooking the runway at Scottsdale Airport and the McDowell Mountains.

Membership perks

Become a VIP

We designed our wine-locker program to complement and elevate the standard cellar program of a locker, a name plate and storage space. Beyond the space and a discount on bottle purchases, wine-locker members enjoy exclusive access to patio reservations, first priority for all events and wine dinners, after-hours offers, discounts on our venue, and other event accommodations.

The membership also includes limited-release and highly sought-after exclusive wines and spirits. We believe our wine-locker program offers perks well above wine storage, and we hope our members appreciate the depth of what it includes.
