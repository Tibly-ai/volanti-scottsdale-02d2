Venues

Three ways to gather above the tarmac

From a 10-person dinner beside a private jet to a 5,000-guest charity gala, every Volanti venue shares one view: the Scottsdale Airport runway with the McDowell Mountains behind it.

Venue 01

Executive Hangars

We have exclusive access to hangars all around the Scottsdale Airpark that can be tailored to any need to ensure your perfect event — from intimate locations for 10-person dinners to 5,000-person charity fundraisers.

Weddings beneath the wings, product launches with the aircraft as your backdrop, galas with full stage, lighting and production — our onsite warehouse and kitchen mean the whole event is built and served right here at the airport.

Contact Us Today

Venue 02

Venue at Volanti

The Venue at Volanti has two meeting rooms, the Stearman and the Thunderbird — named in honor of the airport’s start as a WWII training facility — which may be rented individually or combined with the Thunderbird patio overlooking the Scottsdale Airport tarmac, with breathtaking views of the McDowell Mountains.

Venue amenities

  • 32 classroom-style meeting tables
  • 150 conference-style chairs
  • 100 white Mission chairs
  • Projector and screen
  • A/V system and microphones
  • Covered parking
  • Wi-Fi
  • Private entrance
Contact Us Today

Venue 03

Boardroom

Our boardroom is located inside the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. It seats up to 10 people with direct access to the Scottsdale Airport runway, and comes complete with an 8-foot whiteboard and a TV with HDMI inputs.

Choose from a variety of refreshment options — an in-room mini-fridge stocked with essentials, full-service coffee stations, or full in-meeting dining. Prefer to dine in the restaurant after your meeting? We can book your reservation for lunch, brunch or dinner.

Our story

Fifteen years at Scottsdale Airport

Our team has been onsite at Scottsdale Airport for over 15 years. Our newest restaurant, Volanti, was built on the space of our first restaurant at the airpark. We have grown into all that we are today by doing what we think is most important — building relationships. The City of Scottsdale and all of our neighbors on the airpark have worked with us in building the teams, venues and production tools we have today, and we built relationships throughout the Scottsdale community to help you celebrate not only your grandest occasions but the every day, too.

Volanti Hangar Events houses the only full-service team with a production warehouse and kitchen onsite at the Scottsdale Airport. From the moment our guests walk in the door we work to create unique, personalized event experiences that are unparalleled in the Valley. We are the only event company with everything from our corporate offices to our kitchen and warehouse directly onsite at the airport, and we are proud to be the preferred event company endorsed by the airport director and hangar owners. Every event we host is a customized production you won’t find anywhere else in the Valley.
