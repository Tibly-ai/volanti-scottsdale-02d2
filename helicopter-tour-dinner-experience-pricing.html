Final package pricing depends on party size, total flight duration, and helicopter aircraft capacity.

Factors That Influence Your Helicopter Flight and Dinner Package Cost

When booking a private helicopter tour and dinner experience in Scottsdale, several operational factors shape the total package pricing and overall experience value:

1. Aircraft Capacity and Passenger Group Size

Standard romantic flight packages utilize private helicopters optimized for two passengers. For parties seeking to travel together—such as double dates, family milestones, or small group celebrations—larger helicopters accommodating up to five guests are available. For corporate events or large family gatherings, custom packages can coordinate multiple helicopters taking off on synchronized flight plans.

2. Included Food and Beverage Credit

Every helicopter experience booked with Volanti includes a dedicated $150 credit towards food and beverage during your visit. Guests can apply this credit across the entire menu, whether enjoying fresh seafood, prime steaks, craft cocktails, or champagne during dinner, lunch, or weekend brunch.

3. Flight Plan Duration and Route Schedule

Flight pricing correlates directly with rotor time and flight duration. Private charter flights take off directly from the private tarmac outside the Aviation Business Center, soaring over the Phoenix and Scottsdale Metro area, offering elevated perspectives of the city skyline, local desert terrain, and the McDowell Mountains before returning directly to the airport.

4. Special Event and Package Upgrades

Guests celebrating major milestones can further personalize their experience. From securing specialized reserve wines to integrating large-scale venue spaces through Volanti Hangar Events for post-flight receptions, additional amenities can be integrated into your reservation.

How the Helicopter Flight and Dinner Experience Works

Planning your private flight and dinner package at Scottsdale Airport involves a streamlined check-in process:

  1. Advance Reservation: Select your preferred flight date and time online or by calling 480.657.2426. Because helicopter flights require strict pilot scheduling and aircraft prep, advance booking is required.
  2. Arrival and Check-In: Arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled flight time. Check in on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 N Airport Drive.
  3. Boarding Your Flight: Your flight crew escorts your party down to the private runway. Board your helicopter and take off for a panoramic tour above the Scottsdale and Phoenix metro landscape and the McDowell Mountains.
  4. Runway-Side Dining: Upon landing, walk back up to your reserved table overlooking the tarmac. Spend the remainder of your afternoon or evening utilizing your $150 dining credit while watching aircraft taxi along the runway.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a romantic flight and dinner package typically cost in Scottsdale?

Pricing for a romantic flight and dinner package at Volanti Scottsdale varies based on total flight duration, party size, and aircraft selection. Every flight experience package includes a $150 food and beverage credit that applies directly toward your dining bill at the restaurant.

What is included with the $150 credit during the flight and dinner experience?

The $150 dining credit included with your flight package can be applied toward anything on Volanti's menu during your visit, including starters, prime entrees, desserts, craft cocktails, wine, and champagne.

Do I have to go through TSA airport security before taking the helicopter tour?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. Guests check in directly at the restaurant and proceed straight to the private runway for departure without passing through TSA checkpoints or needing a commercial boarding pass.

How early should we arrive for our helicopter flight and dinner reservation?

Guests are asked to arrive at least 15 minutes prior to their scheduled flight time to check in at the restaurant. This ensures ample time to get situated and escorted down to the tarmac so your flight takes off on schedule.

Can we book a helicopter experience for groups larger than two people?

Yes. While standard date-night packages are built for two passengers, larger helicopters are available for groups of up to five guests. For corporate gatherings or larger private events, Volanti can coordinate custom packages utilizing multiple helicopters.

What happens if there is bad weather on the day of our flight?

Flight departures are subject to change due to inclement weather or pilot availability. Because of scheduling and aircraft reservations, refunds are not available, but rescheduling can be arranged based on pilot and helicopter availability.
