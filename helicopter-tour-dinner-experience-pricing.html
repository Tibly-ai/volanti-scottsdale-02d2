How Much Does a Romantic Flight and Dinner Package Cost in Scottsdale?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 17, 2026

A romantic flight and dinner package at Volanti Scottsdale combines a private helicopter tour over the Phoenix and Scottsdale Metro area with runway-side dining at Scottsdale Airport. Package pricing varies based on helicopter aircraft capacity, total flight duration, and party size, with every reservation including a $150 direct food and beverage credit toward scratch dining, craft cocktails, and wine at Volanti Scottsdale.

Located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport (15000 N Airport Drive, Scottsdale, AZ 85260), Volanti positions guests directly above the runway with views of the McDowell Mountains. Rather than booking an ordinary sightseeing flight in Arizona, an integrated flight-and-dinner package pairs an airborne tour of the Valley with an elevated dining experience. Guests check in directly at the restaurant, walk down to the private runway for takeoff, and return to their reserved table for lunch, dinner, or weekend brunch without ever passing through a TSA security checkpoint.

Factors That Influence Your Flight and Dining Package Cost

When booking a private helicopter tour and dinner experience in Scottsdale, several operational factors shape the total package pricing and value:

1. Aircraft Capacity and Passenger Group Size

Standard romantic flight packages utilize private helicopters optimized for two passengers. For parties seeking to travel together—such as double dates, family milestones, or group celebrations—larger aircraft accommodate groups of up to five guests. Larger aircraft adjust the baseline package cost while allowing the flight experience to be shared across the entire party. For corporate gatherings or large parties, custom packages can coordinate multiple helicopters and custom flight plans.

2. Total Flight Time and Flight Path

The length of your aerial tour across the Phoenix and Scottsdale skies influences total package costs. Flight itineraries take guests over iconic Arizona landmarks, city skylines, and mountain ranges before returning directly to the Scottsdale Airport runway. Longer flight times require additional flight time allocations, which adjust the overall experience cost.

3. Integrated Dining Credit Value

Every flight package comes with a $150 credit towards food and beverage for your visit. Guests can apply this credit directly to anything on Volanti’s chef-driven menu during lunch, weekend brunch, or dinner. Because the dining credit is included directly within the experience package, guests enjoy a single integrated reservation rather than managing separate flight operators and restaurant bookings.

4. Custom Add-Ons and Venue Upgrades

Enhancing your date night or group celebration with private room rentals or sommelier-curated wines will affect total final pricing. Guests looking for exclusive wine access can also explore our wine lockers program for ongoing VIP perks and private cellar storage above the runway.

How the Helicopter Tour & Dinner Experience Works

Booking an integrated flight-and-dinner package in Scottsdale provides a streamlined itinerary managed directly from the airport terminal building.

  1. Reservation & Schedule Selection: Choose a flight date and time that matches your schedule. Flight packages can be paired with weekday lunch (Tuesday–Friday 11 AM–3 PM), weekend brunch (Saturday & Sunday 9 AM–3 PM), or dinner service (Tuesday–Thursday 4:30–9 PM, Friday & Saturday 4:30–10 PM).
  2. Restaurant Check-In: Arrive at Volanti at least 15 minutes ahead of your flight reservation time. Check in at the main hostess stand on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center. Checking in early ensures our team can get you situated and escorted down to our private runway so your flight takes off on time.
  3. Takeoff Over the Valley: Walk down to the private runway for your helicopter tour. Your flight takes in views of the McDowell Mountains and the surrounding Phoenix metro area.
  4. Runway-Side Scratch Dining: After landing back at Scottsdale Airport, head directly up to your reserved table overlooking the tarmac. Spend your $150 food and beverage credit on prime steaks, fresh seafood, craft cocktails, or champagne while watching executive jets taxi past.

The Volanti Experience at Scottsdale Airport

Volanti Scottsdale delivers a scratch kitchen experience centered around seasonal ingredients, craft cocktails, and unobstructed aviation views. Situated above the tarmac, the restaurant features full-service indoor dining, a high-energy bar, and a runway-side outdoor patio.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Much does a romantic flight and dinner package typically cost in Arizona?

A romantic flight and dinner package at Volanti Scottsdale varies based on helicopter capacity (up to 5 guests), total flight duration, and party size. Every reservation includes a private helicopter tour over the Valley paired with a $150 credit towards food and beverage at Volanti Scottsdale.

Do I need to go through security or have a plane ticket to visit Volanti?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport (15000 N Airport Drive). You do not need to be flying out of the airport or go through any TSA security checkpoints to dine at the restaurant or check in for your helicopter flight.

Where do guests park for the helicopter and dinner package?

Guests can park in the large, covered parking lot located directly across from the Aviation Business Center. Parking is free and accessible for all Volanti restaurant, venue, and helicopter experience guests.

Can we book custom flights for larger groups or corporate events?

Yes. While standard packages feature a private helicopter tour for two or larger aircraft for up to five guests, Volanti can craft custom flight packages utilizing multiple helicopters and tailored flight plans for larger groups and corporate gatherings.

What happens if there is bad weather on the day of my scheduled flight?

Flight safety is paramount. Flight times are subject to change due to inclement weather, pilot discretion, or helicopter availability. Because bookings are time-sensitive, refunds are not available; however, rescheduling options are offered based on availability and pilot approval.
