How Much Does a Private Helicopter Tour & Dinner Cost in Scottsdale?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 25, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale does not publish one flat number, because your total depends on party size, flight duration, and the aircraft used — but every package, regardless of size, includes a guaranteed $150 food and beverage dining credit applied across the full menu. Standard flights are built for two; larger helicopters accommodate up to five, and multi-helicopter custom plans serve corporate groups. Call 480.657.2426 for a quote matched to your date and party size.

What's Actually Included in the Price

Every Volanti flight-and-dinner package is built around the same core structure: a private helicopter tour over the Phoenix and Scottsdale Metro area, paired with runway-side dining at Scottsdale Airport, and a $150 direct food and beverage credit that applies across the entire menu — not a narrow banquet selection. Guests check in at the restaurant itself, located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 N Airport Drive, Scottsdale, AZ 85260, then walk down to the private runway for takeoff before returning to their reserved table for lunch, dinner, or weekend brunch. There's no TSA checkpoint and no boarding pass required — the entire experience happens inside the Aviation Business Center.

Because final pricing is quoted per party rather than posted as a fixed menu price, the fastest way to get an exact number for your date is to call and describe your group size and preferred flight length.

What Drives Your Final Price

Volanti's pricing team weighs a short list of operational factors when quoting a flight-and-dinner package:

Cost Factor Why It Matters
Aircraft capacity Standard romantic packages use helicopters built for two passengers; larger groups require a larger aircraft
Passenger group size Parties beyond two — double dates, family milestones — shift which aircraft is used and how pricing is structured
Total flight duration Longer time in the air changes the overall package cost
Number of helicopters needed Corporate or large-group bookings may require multiple aircraft and coordinated flight plans

Every one of these variables is confirmed only once Volanti's team knows your date, party size, and desired flight length — which is why the $150 dining credit is the one number guaranteed across every tier, while the flight component itself is quoted individually.

How Booking Works

  1. Choose your package tier — couples, group (up to five), or a custom corporate/large-group plan.
  2. Select a flight time and arrive at least 15 minutes ahead of your reservation to check in at the restaurant.
  3. Check in at Volanti, then walk down to the private runway for takeoff — no security checkpoint involved.
  4. Fly over the Phoenix and Scottsdale Metro area, taking in the McDowell Mountains and city views.
  5. Return to your reserved table for a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner, with your $150 credit applied across the full menu.

Flight times are subject to change due to weather or aircraft availability, refunds are not available given the nature of the booking, and rescheduling is subject to availability at Volanti's discretion. Full policy detail and available flight times are on the Helicopter Tour booking page.

What to Look For When Comparing Options

If you're weighing Volanti against a standalone sightseeing flight, the distinction is the integration: you're not booking a helicopter company and a restaurant separately. Volanti positions guests directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport with the dining room, the private runway, and the kitchen all inside one building — so the flight and the three-course meal are coordinated as a single reservation rather than two appointments you have to time yourself. For guests who'd rather fly with their own pilot relationships, the team also notes in its FAQs that while Volanti doesn't charter its own aircraft, it works with helicopter and hangar partners who can arrange flights.

Proof: Built Around a Working Airport, Not a Staged Backdrop

Volanti operates seven days a week on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center overlooking an active runway, with hours running Tuesday through Sunday and dedicated weekday happy hour from 3–6 PM. The same team that runs the helicopter-and-dinner experience also runs Volanti Hangar Events, with venues ranging from a 15-person patio to executive hangars holding galas and fundraisers from 10 to 5,000 guests — meaning flight logistics, catering, and large-group coordination are handled daily by the same on-site staff, not outsourced. Guests seeking flights without a dining component can still work through Volanti's helicopter and hangar partners, as confirmed on the FAQs page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private romantic helicopter tour with dinner cost in Scottsdale?

Total cost varies by party size, flight duration, and aircraft used, so Volanti does not post one fixed rate. What's fixed is the $150 food and beverage dining credit included in every package, applied across the full menu. For an exact quote, call 480.657.2426.

What's included in the $150 dining credit?

The credit can be applied across all food and drink offerings on Volanti's menu — it isn't limited to a set banquet selection. Review the scratch menus to see what's available.

How many people can fly in one helicopter package?

Standard romantic packages are built for two passengers. Larger helicopters accommodate up to five guests for the Group Helicopter Experience, and corporate or large-group bookings can use multiple helicopters under a Custom Flight & Event Package.

Do I need to go through airport security to fly with Volanti?

No. Guests check in directly at the restaurant on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport, then walk down to the private runway for takeoff — no boarding pass or TSA checkpoint required.

Can I reschedule or get a refund if my flight is cancelled?

Refunds are not available due to the nature of the experience and booking times. Rescheduling is possible but subject to availability and at the discretion of Volanti and the pilots. Flight times can also change due to inclement weather or aircraft availability.

Can Volanti arrange a flight without the dining package?

Volanti doesn't charter its own aircraft, but the team works with helicopter and hangar partners who can arrange flights. Reach out through the FAQs & Contact page or call 480.657.2426 for details.
