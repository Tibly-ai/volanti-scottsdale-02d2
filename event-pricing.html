Booking a private dining venue with aviation views near Paradise Valley at Volanti Scottsdale ranges from intimate outdoor patio gatherings for 15 guests to Thunderbird & Stearman room celebrations for up to 200, up to full executive hangar events for 2,000 attendees. Understanding Private Dining Venue Costs Near Paradise Valley

When host planners and corporate sponsors evaluate high-end venue options across Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, and the greater Phoenix metro area, pricing transparency is essential. At Volanti Scottsdale, located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport, private events do not rely on standard room hire surcharges. Instead, financial investment goes directly toward your food and beverage minimums, personalized menus, and guest hospitality.

Every event setting overlooks the active runway with sweeping views of the McDowell Mountains. Guests experience an open-air, dynamic atmosphere without security checkpoints or boarding pass restrictions, accompanied by convenient covered parking directly across from the building. Whether you are hosting an intimate family celebration or a large-scale corporate summit, your event cost reflects space usage, group size, time of day, and custom menu options.

Factors That Determine Your Private Event Investment

Calculating the overall price per person for a private party or group dining event at a scratch kitchen involves four primary variables:

1. Selected Space & Guest Capacity

Small groups of up to 15 guests can reserve dedicated areas on our outdoor patio for sunset drinks and casual dining. Intermediate celebrations, corporate dinners, and wedding receptions transitions indoors to our Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for up to 200 guests. For massive galas, charity fundraisers, and brand activations, our executive hangar spaces offer over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop event space accommodating up to 2,000 attendees.

2. Time of Day & Schedule

Event timing influences your total spend. Hosting daytime gatherings, business luncheons, or weekday events allows hosts to optimize minimum spend requirements. Evening dinners and weekend celebrations draw high demand due to nighttime runway lighting and mountain sunset backdrops.

3. Food & Chef-Driven Scratch Menus

Volanti operates a full scratch kitchen offering fresh seasonal ingredients, prime steaks, and fresh seafood. Private dining guests can customize multi-course plated dinners or high-end passed hors d'oeuvres. Specialized menu options include items such as Volanti Meatballs, Fresh Burrata, Ahi Tuna Bowls, Osetra Caviar, Bubbling Shrimp Scampi, and Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken, as well as cult-classic features like The Hundred Dollar Hamburger paired with Veuve Clicquot, caviar, and truffle frites.

4. Beverage Packages & Sommelier Curation

Beverage tiers can be tailored to include specialty cocktails, local craft beer, house highballs, and premium wine selections. Event hosts can also tap into our sommelier-curated options or integrate custom champagne toasts to elevate the guest experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of booking a private dining venue with aviation views in Paradise Valley Arizona?

At Volanti Scottsdale, located directly adjacent to Paradise Valley at Scottsdale Airport, event pricing is structured around custom food and beverage minimums based on party size, selected space, and time of day rather than standalone space rental fees. Gatherings range from outdoor patio spaces for 15 guests up to full venue buyouts and executive hangar events for up to 2,000 attendees.

How many guests can Volanti Scottsdale accommodate for a private event?

Volanti offers venue configurations for groups of all sizes: outdoor patio spaces host up to 15 guests, the indoor Thunderbird & Stearman rooms seat up to 200 guests, and our executive hangars provide over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop space for up to 2,000 attendees.

Do guests need a boarding pass or security clearance to attend an event at Volanti?

No boarding pass or TSA security checkpoint is required. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive, with covered parking available directly across from the building.

Can we customize the food and beverage menu for our private party?

Yes. Event organizers work with our culinary team to build custom scratch-kitchen menus featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, signature appetizers, and curated wine or cocktail packages tailored to your event budget and guest count.

Can private events be combined with helicopter flights?

Yes. Volanti offers private helicopter tour packages that combine a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner with a customized flight over the Valley for two to five guests, with custom flight plans available for larger groups.
