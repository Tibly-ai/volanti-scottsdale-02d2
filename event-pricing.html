Venue Rental & Private Party Costs at Scottsdale Airport

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 22, 2026

Private event and venue rental pricing at Volanti Scottsdale is structured around custom food and beverage minimums, space requirements, and party size. Hosting an event at our Scottsdale Airport venue ranges from intimate patio gatherings for 15 guests to full dinner celebrations for 200 in our Thunderbird & Stearman rooms, up to mega-scale hangar fundraisers for 2,000 attendees across our executive hangars.

How Much Does It Cost to Venue Rent a Rooftop Restaurant for a Party?

When planning a rooftop or runway-side event, venue rental costs typically depend on whether you are booking a partial space, a dedicated private dining room, or a total venue buyout. At Volanti Scottsdale, pricing is built around food and beverage minimums alongside square footage needs rather than arbitrary space fees, ensuring your budget directly funds your menu, craft cocktails, and guest experience.

For smaller private groups, our outdoor patio space accommodates gatherings of 15 guests looking for casual happy hour drinks or a seated group dinner. Larger corporate galas, elevated milestone celebrations, and weddings transition into our indoor Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for up to 200 guests. For large-scale summits, charity galas, and corporate brand activations, our executive hangar spaces provide over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop space for up to 2,000 attendees.

Every event taking place above the runway at Scottsdale Airport includes direct views of the McDowell Mountains and private aviation traffic, eliminating the standard, closed-off banquet room atmosphere in favor of a dynamic venue backdrop.

Factors That Determine Average Price Per Person for a Private Party Menu at a Luxury Restaurant

Calculating the average price per person for a private party menu involves evaluating guest count, time of day, food selections, and beverage packages. At a scratch kitchen like Volanti Scottsdale, private dining menus reflect seasonal ingredients, prime steaks, fresh seafood, and custom bar programs.

1. Selected Menu Style and Course Structure

The style of service directly dictates overall culinary spending:

  • Plated Dinners: Multi-course plated dinners featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house specialties carry a higher cost per person due to direct kitchen staffing and precision timing.
  • Reception Style & Passed Apps: Cocktail-style receptions featuring passed small bites and station setups—such as Short Rib Tacos, Volanti Meatballs, Tiny Tuna Tartare, BBQ Pork Sliders, and the Take Off Trio with house focaccia—offer flexible spending per guest while encouraging movement.
  • Brunch & Lunch Gatherings: Hosting daytime events during lunch or weekend brunch hours allows hosts to utilize lower daytime food and beverage minimums compared to prime weekend dinner slots.

2. Beverage Programs and Wine Pairings

Bar service represents a major portion of event budgeting. Private parties can select hosted open bar packages, curated wine pairings, or drink tickets featuring specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, and champagne options. Guests utilizing our on-site wine lockers program or sommelier service can also integrate exclusive, limited-release wines directly into their private dining experience.

3. Space Allocation and Buyout Requirements

Reserving specific sections during peak operating hours requires meeting calculated food and beverage minimums. A partial room reservation for 15 to 20 guests carries significantly lower minimum spend commitments than securing total access to the second-floor dining room or expanding into our executive hangars.
