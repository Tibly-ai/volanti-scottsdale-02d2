How Much Does a Private Dining Venue with Aviation Views Cost in Phoenix, AZ?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 25, 2026

At Volanti Scottsdale, there is no flat room-rental fee for private dining or corporate events — cost is set entirely by a custom food and beverage minimum that scales with your guest count, venue choice, and time of day. Serving Scottsdale, Phoenix, and Paradise Valley, groups run from 15 guests on the outdoor patio to 2,000 guests in the executive hangars, with 100% of your spend going toward scratch-kitchen food and craft cocktails.

What Determines the Cost of a Private Event at Volanti Scottsdale

Volanti doesn't charge separately for the room. Instead, your financial commitment is built around a food and beverage minimum tied to four variables:

  • Guest count — from an intimate 15-person patio gathering up to a 2,000-guest executive hangar production
  • Venue selection — patio, the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms, or the executive hangars each carry different minimums based on size and setup
  • Time of day — lunch, dinner, and evening reception timing affect menu structure and staffing
  • Menu selections — chef-driven scratch menus and craft cocktail programs are built to your specifications

Because there's no standard room-hire surcharge, every dollar you commit funds the actual dining experience — not overhead. This structure is consistent whether you're booking from Scottsdale, from Phoenix proper, or from Paradise Valley.

Private Dining Venue Pricing in Phoenix, Arizona

For Phoenix-area companies and families booking a private dining venue with aviation views, the cost structure at Volanti is identical regardless of which part of the Valley you're coming from: a food and beverage minimum replaces any room rental charge. Phoenix groups most often book the outdoor patio (up to 15 guests) for smaller gatherings or the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms (up to 200 guests) for corporate dinners and receptions, with the minimum set by party size and menu rather than a separate venue fee. Located at 15000 North Airport Drive on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, the drive from central Phoenix puts you at a runway-view venue with covered parking and no security checkpoint required.

Private Dining Venue Pricing in Paradise Valley, Arizona

Paradise Valley clients booking aviation-view private dining at Volanti follow the same food-and-beverage-minimum model — there is no separate room charge layered on top. What changes for Paradise Valley groups is typically scale: this market has used Volanti's executive hangars for large-format events, including an opening celebration for The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie, which brought over 1,000 guests into the Volanti Hangar alongside a Bombardier jet staged against the McDowell Mountains. That event is public proof that the hangar tier, which the events page lists as accommodating galas and fundraisers from 10 to 5,000 guests, is built for Paradise Valley-scale productions, not just Scottsdale dinners.

How Much a Private Business Lunch Costs at Scottsdale Airport's Runway-View Restaurant

A private business lunch at Volanti is also priced by food and beverage minimum rather than a seating or room fee, with the smallest formal private option being the on-site Boardroom, which seats up to 10 people with direct runway access, an 8-foot whiteboard, and a TV with HDMI inputs. Groups can choose an in-room mini-fridge stocked with essentials, a full-service coffee station, or full in-meeting dining catered from the Volanti kitchen — or simply book a table in the restaurant for lunch, served Tuesday through Friday, 11 AM–3 PM, and finish the meeting over a reservation. Larger business lunches of up to 200 move into the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms, still priced on the same minimum-spend model.

How Booking Works

  1. Choose your venue tier — patio, boardroom, Thunderbird & Stearman, or executive hangar, based on guest count and formality.
  2. Set your date and time — lunch, dinner, or evening reception timing shapes the menu and minimum.
  3. Build your menu with the Volanti kitchen — scratch-cooked, chef-driven menus and craft cocktails, customized to your group.
  4. Your food and beverage minimum is confirmed — this is your total financial commitment; there is no added room-hire charge.
  5. Pair it with an experience — groups can add the Helicopter Tour, which includes a customized flight for two (larger helicopters available for up to five) paired with a three-course meal and a $150 credit toward food and beverage, or reserve a Wine Locker for VIP perks like first access to patio reservations and event priority.

To start the conversation, reach the events team directly or explore the full Volanti Hangar Events lineup and the restaurant's current menus to plan your food and beverage selections in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to book a private dining venue with aviation views in Phoenix, Arizona?

There is no separate room fee — your cost is a food and beverage minimum set by guest count, venue (patio, Thunderbird & Stearman, or executive hangars), time of day, and menu selections, with every dollar applied directly to chef-driven food and craft cocktails.

How much does a private business lunch cost at top-view restaurants in Phoenix?

Private business lunches at Volanti follow the same minimum-spend model as larger events. The smallest formal option is the Boardroom, seating up to 10 with in-room dining or coffee-station choices, while groups up to 200 can book the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for a catered lunch.

How much does it cost to book a private dining venue with aviation views in Paradise Valley, Arizona?

Paradise Valley clients pay the same food-and-beverage-minimum structure as any Volanti booking. For scale reference, the executive hangars have hosted events for over 1,000 guests, including the opening celebration for The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie.

Does Volanti charge a separate room rental fee on top of the food and beverage minimum?

No. The venue commitment is 100% applied toward food, beverage, and hospitality — there is no additional room-hire surcharge at any capacity tier, from the patio to the executive hangars.

What is the largest event Volanti can host?

The executive hangars provide over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop space and can accommodate galas and fundraisers from 10 up to 5,000 guests, with the airport-facing pricing tier referenced for groups up to 2,000.

Can I add a helicopter flight to a private dining event?

Yes. The Helicopter Tour pairs a customized flight around the Valley for two (larger helicopters available for groups up to five) with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner, and includes a $150 credit toward food and beverage.
