How Much Does Luxury Corporate Event Catering Cost Near Scottsdale Airport?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 9, 2026

Luxury corporate event catering and private dining costs near Scottsdale Airport are structured around custom food and beverage minimums and venue space requirements rather than arbitrary rental fees. At Volanti Scottsdale, located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, your financial investment directly funds your scratch-kitchen menu and craft cocktails for events ranging from patio gatherings to large executive hangar productions.

Understanding Event Catering Costs at Scottsdale Airport

When event planners, host planners, and corporate sponsors evaluate high-end venue and catering options across Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, and the greater Phoenix metro area, pricing transparency is essential. At Volanti Scottsdale, private events do not rely on standard room hire surcharges. Instead, financial investment goes directly toward food and beverage minimums, personalized scratch-kitchen menus, and guest hospitality.

Every event setting overlooks the active runway at Scottsdale Airport with sweeping views of the McDowell Mountains. Guests experience an open-air, dynamic atmosphere without security checkpoints or boarding pass restrictions, accompanied by convenient covered parking directly across from the building. Whether hosting an intimate corporate team lunch, an executive dinner, or a large-scale corporate summit, overall event investment reflects space usage, guest capacity, time of day, and custom menu selections.

Key Factors That Determine Corporate Event Catering Investment

Calculating the total financial investment for corporate event catering or group dining involves several primary variables:

1. Selected Space and Guest Capacity

Event settings scale based on your total attendee count and layout requirements:

  • Outdoor Patio Space: Accommodates small groups for sunset drinks, team celebrations, and casual dining.
  • Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms: Provides dedicated indoor dining space with scratch menus for corporate dinners, presentations, and receptions.
  • Executive Hangars: Offers expansive combined hangar, runway, and rooftop event space for large galas, fundraisers, product launches, and major corporate events.

2. Scratch-Kitchen Catering and Custom Menus

Because Volanti operates a chef-driven scratch kitchen, catering menus feature fresh, seasonal flavors, prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house specialties. Packages can be structured around plated dinners, multi-course dining, or shared small plates. Custom menu choices directly dictate your overall food and beverage spend.

3. Beverage Programs and Bar Formats

Event hosts can select custom beverage structures, including specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, house wine, and champagne toasts. For specialized wine pairings, event planners can coordinate with the in-house sommelier or utilize wine locker arrangements for VIP hosting.

4. Direct Aircraft and Jet Catering Logistics

For corporate travel and aviation clients operating out of KSDL FBOs, jet catering brings the full kitchen menu directly to your aircraft. Deliveries are tailored to private flight itineraries, ensuring high-end dining onboard without compromising quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does luxury corporate event catering cost near the Scottsdale airport?

At Volanti Scottsdale, event catering costs are determined by custom food and beverage minimums and space requirements rather than flat venue rental fees. Total costs depend on your selected venue space, guest count, time of day, and custom scratch-kitchen menu options.

Yes. Volanti Jet Catering brings the complete scratch-kitchen menu directly to aircraft at Scottsdale Airport, tailoring meals and delivery schedules for private flight crews and passengers.
