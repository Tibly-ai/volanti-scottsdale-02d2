Volanti Scottsdale vs J&G Steakhouse: How to Choose

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 22, 2026

Choosing between Volanti Scottsdale and J&G Steakhouse comes down to your preference for location, atmosphere, culinary focus, and event capabilities in the Greater Phoenix area. Volanti Scottsdale delivers a scratch kitchen dining experience located directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport with panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains. Diners seeking runway-side steak, seafood, craft cocktails, weekend brunch, or custom private helicopter dining experiences will find Volanti tailored to aviation-themed elevated dining.

When deciding where to reserve a table for dinner, host a corporate gathering, or celebrate a milestone, evaluating specific operational criteria ensures your chosen venue matches your occasion's exact requirements.

Key Decision Factors for Upscale Scottsdale Dining

Selecting an upscale venue in Scottsdale involves weighing distinct venue characteristics. Here are the primary factors to evaluate when considering your options:

1. Setting and Scenic Views

The physical environment establishes the tone for your dining experience. Consider whether your party prefers high-altitude mountain scenery or interactive, aviation-focused views. Volanti Scottsdale is positioned on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive. Every table looks directly out over the active runway alongside views of the McDowell Mountains, allowing guests to watch private jets taxi and take off while dining without requiring a boarding pass or security checkpoint.

2. Menu Philosophy and Scratch Kitchen Offerings

Examine the menu offerings to ensure they suit the dietary preferences and tastes of your entire group. Volanti features a chef-driven scratch kitchen centered on prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house specialties. Signature dishes range from Ahi Tuna Bowls and Osetra Caviar to Tagliatelle Wild Mushroom Pasta and Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken, alongside specialty craft cocktails, local craft beer, and curated wines.

3. Unique Experiential Packages

If your meal is tied to a date night, special celebration, or guest outing, explore whether the restaurant provides interactive packages beyond traditional table service. Volanti offers signature experiential offerings including:

  • Helicopter Tour & Dinner Packages: A private flight around the Valley for two paired with a three-course meal and a $150 food and beverage credit.
  • Private Wine Locker Program: On-site wine storage curated with an in-house sommelier, granting members VIP patio reservation access, event priority, and exclusive wine allocations.
  • The Hundred Dollar Hamburger: An aviation-inspired tradition featuring a premium burger paired with Veuve Clicquot, caviar, and truffle frites.

4. Event Spaces and Group Capacities

For group celebrations, dinners, or corporate functions, inspect the venue's space flexibility and capacity parameters. Evaluating scalable layouts ensures your group is accommodated comfortably:

  • Small Group Celebrations: Outdoor patio space suitable for intimate gatherings of 15.
  • Mid-Sized Private Dinners: The Thunderbird & Stearman rooms designed for seated dinners of up to 200 guests.
  • Large-Scale Functions & Fundraisers: Over 30,000 square feet of venue space across Executive Hangars capable of hosting events for up to 2,000 attendees.

5. Arrival Logistics and Accessibility

Evaluate parking convenience, entry protocols, and transit access for your party. Located in North Scottsdale, Volanti offers covered parking directly across from the Aviation Business Center with seamless, second-floor elevator access—eliminating long valet lines or complex resort walking paths.

Evaluating Your Options in the Scottsdale Market

When comparing Volanti Scottsdale to J&G Steakhouse or other local fine-dining establishments, use this structured checklist to guide your final choice:

  • Verify Menu Diversity: Review current online options to confirm the menu provides balanced choices across steak, seafood, vegetarian plates, and shared appetizers. Check Volanti’s dining and happy hour menus to view current culinary offerings.
  • Confirm Timing & Reservation Policies: Verify operating hours for specific services like lunch, weekday happy hour, or weekend brunch. Parties of 8 or more at Volanti are encouraged to call direct at 480.657.2426 for coordinated group seating.
  • Review Group Catering & Flight Services: If traveling via private aviation or hosting off-site events, evaluate specialized catering programs. Volanti Jet Catering delivers restaurant menus directly to aircraft on the runway.
  • Assess Event Ambience: Match the venue layout to your desired tone—whether that is an intimate patio table at sunset, a structured private dining room, or a large hangar celebration. Explore Volanti’s event and venue options for private gathering details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an airport ticket or security clearance to dine at Volanti Scottsdale?

No boarding pass, ticket, or airport security checkpoint is required. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive and is open to the public for walk-ins and reservations.

What hours is Happy Hour available at Volanti Scottsdale?

Happy hour runs weekdays from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM at the bar and outdoor patio, featuring small bites, house highballs, specialty cocktails, local craft beer, house wine, and half-off champagne bottles.

How does the Helicopter Tour and Dinner package work?

The package pairs a customized flight around the Valley for two (with larger helicopters available for up to five guests) with a three-course meal at Volanti. The package includes a $150 credit toward food and beverage from the full menu. Bookings can be coordinated via Volanti's helicopter experience portal.

How do I reserve a table for a party larger than eight guests?

For groups of eight or more, guests should call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426 to arrange seating, dedicated private dining rooms, or customized event menus.

What perks come with the Volanti Wine Locker membership?

Members receive on-site cellar storage curated by an in-house sommelier, priority access for patio reservations, VIP invitations to wine dinners, after-hours offers, venue discounts, and access to exclusive wine and spirit allocations. Interested diners can apply through Volanti's wine locker application.
