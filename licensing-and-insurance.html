Licensing, Insurance, and Credentials at Volanti Scottsdale

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 26, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale operates as a fully licensed and insured hospitality establishment located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport (15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, Arizona 85260). The restaurant serves Scottsdale, Phoenix, and the broader Arizona market, adhering to all state and local commercial food service, health, safety, and aviation-adjacent building requirements.

Venue Credentials and Aviation Access

Operating inside an active airport facility requires strict adherence to municipal, state, and aviation authority standards. Volanti Scottsdale is situated above the runway within the Aviation Business Center, providing a dedicated dining and event venue that operates seamlessly alongside active aircraft operations.

  • No Boarding Pass Required: Guests access the restaurant directly on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center without passing through TSA security checkpoints or needing flight documentation.
  • Commercial Property Compliance: The venue complies with all local building codes, structural fire safety protocols, and commercial occupancy standards required for airport-adjacent food service and large-scale gatherings.
  • Covered Guest Parking: Dedicated covered parking is located directly across from the Aviation Business Center entrance for convenient accessibility.

Dining, Event, and Off-Site Service Capabilities

Volanti Scottsdale maintains complete operational coverage for multiple distinct service models across its restaurant space, private event rooms, and off-site aviation catering programs.

1. Restaurant & Patio Dining

Volanti operates a full scratch kitchen offering lunch, dinner, weekend brunch, and weekday happy hour.

  • Lunch: Tuesday through Friday, 11 AM – 3 PM
  • Dinner: Tuesday through Thursday, 4:30 PM – 9 PM; Friday & Saturday, 4:30 PM – 10 PM
  • Brunch: Saturday & Sunday, 9 AM – 3 PM
  • Happy Hour: Weekdays, 3 PM – 6 PM on the patio and at the bar

2. Private Events & Hangar Buyouts

The dedicated events team manages gatherings ranging from small patio celebrations to full-scale hangar galas.

  • Patio Gatherings: Hosted on the outdoor deck for groups around 15 guests.
  • Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms: Indoor private dining configurations capable of hosting dinner celebrations for up to 200 guests.
  • Executive Hangars: Over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop space capable of hosting fundraisers and events for up to 2,000 guests.

3. In-Flight & Jet Catering

Volanti Jet Catering operates directly out of the Scottsdale Airport facility, delivering customized scratch-kitchen menus straight to private aircraft on the tarmac.

4. Aerial Experiences

In partnership with licensed local aviation operators, Volanti offers a custom helicopter tour and dining package. Flight operations depart from the private runway and are subject to pilot discretion, aircraft availability, and weather safety guidelines.

Verifying Hospitality & Local Operating Standards

When visiting or booking private events at Scottsdale Airport, guests and event planners are encouraged to verify operational credentials.

  1. Physical Location: Verify the address at 15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, AZ 85260, inside the Aviation Business Center.
  2. Direct Contact: Confirm reservations, private dining guidelines, or hangar buyouts directly by calling 480.657.2426.
  3. Health & Safety Compliance: Maricopa County Environmental Services conducts routine inspection reviews for commercial kitchens operating within the Scottsdale jurisdiction.
  4. Flight Safety Regulations: All aerial packages, including the private helicopter experience, adhere strictly to FAA flight safety protocols managed by certified flight crews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a boarding pass or ticket to enter Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti Scottsdale is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center and is open to the public. You do not need a ticket, boarding pass, or security clearance to enter and dine.

Where do guests park when visiting the restaurant?

Covered parking is located directly across from the Aviation Business Center building at 15000 North Airport Drive.

What are the operational hours for dining and happy hour?

Lunch is served Tuesday through Friday from 11 AM to 3 PM. Dinner is served Tuesday through Thursday from 4:30 PM to 9 PM, and Friday and Saturday from 4:30 PM to 10 PM. Weekend brunch is available Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM. Weekday happy hour runs from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio and bar.

How do I make a reservation for a large group?

Walk-ins are always welcome. For standard dining reservations, guests can book online. For parties of 8 or more, or to discuss private event spaces, call the venue directly at 480.657.2426.

Does Volanti serve markets outside of Scottsdale?

Yes. Volanti serves guests and event hosts across Scottsdale, Phoenix, and the wider Arizona area, including specialized jet catering directly to private aircraft operating out of Scottsdale Airport.
