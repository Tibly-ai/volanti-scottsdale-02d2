Factors Influencing Executive Event & Business Dining Costs

Understanding the investment required for executive dining or a private room buyout in the Phoenix and Scottsdale market involves evaluating spatial allocations, time frames, and customized culinary requirements.

1. Guest Count & Space Requirements

Group size dictates room minimums and dedicated venue space allocation. Smaller corporate team outings can be hosted in our 10-person boardroom or on our runway-side patio for parties up to 15. Mid-sized corporate presentations and executive luncheons for up to 200 attendees utilize the dedicated Thunderbird and Stearman rooms. Large-scale corporate summits or galas utilize our executive hangars, accommodating up to 2,000 guests across more than 30,000 square feet of space.

2. Dining Format & Scratch Kitchen Menu Selection

Volanti operates a scratch, chef-driven kitchen. Food and beverage spend fluctuates based on whether your event calls for full plated entrees, corporate coffee breaks, or shared small plates. Corporate menus feature prime steaks, fresh seafood, and signature dishes alongside crafted beverages.

  • To Share: Options include Serrano Pesto Focaccia Bread, Volanti Meatballs (veal, sirloin, pork sausage), Fresh Burrata, and the Take Off Trio. Premium items like Osetra Caviar (30g with cucumber onion dip and house chips), Bubbling Shrimp Scampi, or Ahi Tuna Bowl can be added for executive tasting courses.
  • Salads & Entrees: Fresh salads like the Zulu, Sesame Ginger, Cardini, or Peruvian can be paired with grilled proteins (chicken, steak, salmon, or king prawns). Plated entrees range from Wild Mushroom Pasta and Bubbling Shrimp Pasta to Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken.
  • Beverage Packages: Signature cocktails (such as the Hugo Spritz or Summer Solstice), local craft beer, zero-proof mocktails (like the Blackberry Refresher), and extensive wine selections directly impact final menu pricing.

3. Audio/Visual & Meeting Amenities

For formal business presentations, private space rentals can include fully integrated technology assets. The venue provides 32 classroom-style meeting tables, 150 conference-style chairs, 100 white Mission chairs, a projector and screen, a complete A/V system with microphones, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a private entrance to ensure uninterrupted corporate proceedings.

Corporate Dining Logistics & Arrival at Scottsdale Airport

Hosting a business lunch near Phoenix requires frictionless logistics for off-site attendees. Situated at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Volanti sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center.

  • No TSA or Airport Security Checkpoint Required: Guests access the restaurant and private event rooms directly through the Aviation Business Center without needing a boarding pass or clearing airport security.
  • Dedicated Covered Parking: A large covered parking structure is located directly across from the main entrance, shared only with the Aviation Business Center, City of Scottsdale Airport staff, venue guests, and U.S. Customs.
  • Proximity to Business Hubs: Located within the Scottsdale Airpark corridor, the venue is a short six-minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter and minutes from Kierland Commons, making it easily accessible for executive teams traveling across Phoenix and Scottsdale.

Executive Dining & Aviation Experiences

For corporate hosts looking to elevate client entertainment beyond a standard private room, Volanti integrates specialized aviation offerings directly into business dining plans:

  • Helicopter Tour & Flight Packages: Executive bookers can pair a three-course business lunch, brunch, or dinner with a private helicopter flight around the Valley for two to five guests, including a $150 food and beverage credit towards the meal. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private business lunch cost at top view restaurants in Phoenix?

Private business lunch costs depend on your group size, room minimums, venue space selection, and custom scratch menu choices. Space options range from an intimate 10-person boardroom or a 15-guest runway patio gathering up to private room buyouts for 200 guests in the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms, or large-scale hangar events hosting up to 2,000 attendees.

Do business lunch guests have to go through airport security at Volanti?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. Guests enter directly through the main building without needing a boarding pass or passing through TSA security checkpoints.

What venue spaces are available for corporate meetings and lunches?

Volanti offers four primary venue options: a 10-person Boardroom featuring an 8-foot whiteboard and TV with HDMI inputs; a runway-side Patio for up to 15 guests; the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for up to 200 guests; and Executive Hangars offering over 30,000 square feet of space for up to 2,000 guests.

Where do clients and corporate guests park when attending a business lunch?

Guests park in the large covered parking lot situated directly across from the Aviation Business Center. The lot serves restaurant patrons, corporate venue guests, airport staff, and U.S. Customs.

Can we combine a corporate lunch with a helicopter flight or private event package?

Yes. Volanti offers a Helicopter Tour & Flight Package that combines a three-course meal with a private flight over the Valley for groups of two to five, including a $150 credit towards food and beverage. Custom flight itineraries involving multiple helicopters can also be designed for larger corporate groups.

Does Volanti offer to-go or third-party delivery options for off-site corporate lunches?

No. Volanti operates a scratch kitchen prioritizing fresh, seasonal offerings for on-site dining and cannot accommodate to-go or regular takeout orders. For non-aviation off-site dining, traditional takeout is unavailable; however, direct aircraft dining is serviced through Volanti Jet Catering. Guests with limited time for lunch may utilize bar seating for quick bites.
