Factors Influencing Executive Event & Business Dining Costs

Understanding the investment required for executive dining or a private room buyout in the Phoenix and Scottsdale market involves evaluating spatial allocations, time frames, and customized culinary requirements.

1. Guest Count & Space Requirements

Group size dictates room minimums and dedicated venue space allocation. Smaller corporate team outings can reserve tables or gather on the outdoor patio for up to 15 guests. Mid-sized organizations requiring dedicated private buyouts can utilize the indoor Thunderbird and Stearman rooms for up to 200 guests. For enterprise-level events, over 30,000 square feet of hangar and runway space accommodates up to 2,000 guests.

2. Time Frames & Scheduling

Lunch events scheduled during daytime hours allow executives to maximize productivity while enjoying a chef-driven menu. Volanti offers lunch service Tuesday through Friday from 11 AM to 3 PM. Groups looking for post-meeting transitions can seamlessly transition into weekday happy hour from 3 PM to 6 PM, which features specialty cocktails, craft beer, and half-off champagne bottles on the patio as aircraft taxi past.

3. Menu Customization & Culinary Services

Volanti operates a scratch kitchen focusing on fresh, seasonal offerings. Corporate event dining can be tailored around globally inspired menus built from the ground up. Menu selections range from shareable starters and fresh salads to prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house desserts. Custom coffee stations, stocked mini-fridges, and tailored beverage packages further refine the total event budget.

4. Meeting Technology & Dedicated Amenities

For executive board meetings, clear presentation technology is essential. The Boardroom inside the Aviation Business Center features direct access to the runway along with an 8-foot whiteboard and a TV with HDMI inputs. Larger corporate presentations in the Stearman and Thunderbird rooms utilize integrated venue amenities including a projector, projection screen, full A/V system, and microphones.

Executive Dining Venues Comparison at Volanti Scottsdale

To help you choose the right setting for your upcoming corporate lunch or private executive gathering, review our primary indoor and outdoor event options:

The Boardroom

Designed specifically for high-level executive meetings, board discussions, and confidential client presentations. Located within the Aviation Business Center, this space comfortably seats up to 10 guests. It provides immediate access to the runway and includes a whiteboard, HDMI-equipped television, and options for full in-meeting catering, stocked mini-fridges, or full-service coffee setups.

Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms

Ideal for larger business luncheons, company-wide meetings, corporate award ceremonies, and evening dinners. Named in honor of the airport's origins as a WWII training facility, these rooms can be booked individually or combined. They accommodate up to 200 guests and offer direct access to the Thunderbird patio overlooking the tarmac. Setup options include classroom-style meeting tables and conference chairs alongside integrated A/V facilities.

Executive Hangars

For large-scale corporate summits, product unveilings, and charity fundraisers, Volanti Hangar Events manages access to massive hangar spaces surrounding the airfield. With over 30,000 square feet of combined hangar, runway, and rooftop event space, these venues handle large crowds with custom staging, lighting, and full catering serviced directly from the Volanti scratch kitchen.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private business lunch cost at top view restaurants in Phoenix?

Private business lunch pricing at Volanti Scottsdale depends on your group size, chosen venue space, room minimums, and selected menu items. Options range from small team lunches on the outdoor patio to private room buyouts in the Thunderbird and Stearman rooms or fully catered meetings in the Boardroom.

Do guests need to pass through airport security to attend a corporate lunch at Volanti?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. It is open to the public, and guests do not need a boarding pass or security screening to access the restaurant or private meeting rooms. Covered parking is available directly across from the building.

What are the meeting space options for small board meetings?

The Boardroom inside the Aviation Business Center seats up to 10 guests. It features direct runway views, an 8-foot whiteboard, a TV with HDMI inputs, and options for in-room mini-fridge stocking, coffee stations, or full in-meeting dining.

Can custom catering menus be created for corporate dietary needs?

Yes. Volanti operates a chef-driven scratch kitchen that builds globally inspired menus from the ground up. Our culinary team can customize catering options for coffee breaks, plated corporate lunches, or multi-course dinners. For full menu options, view our restaurant menus page.

Is outdoor seating available for private executive lunches?

Group patio gatherings for up to 15 guests can be hosted outdoors overlooking the runway. Because patio seating is in high demand, standard patio dining for smaller walk-in parties is capped at an hour and a half for parties of four or fewer, and two hours for parties of five or more.

How do we reserve a private meeting space or corporate room buyout?

You can inquire about private room availability, board meetings, and full venue buyouts by contacting our events team through our website or by calling 480.657.2426. For general dining reservations for smaller groups, visit our dining and reservations page.
