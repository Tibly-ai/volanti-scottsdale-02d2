# Best Private Dining Rooms for Executive Business Meetings in North Scottsdale with Views

Volanti Scottsdale delivers executive business meeting venues overlooking the runway at Scottsdale Airport, framed by views of the McDowell Mountains. Operating from the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with dedicated runway-side venue spaces, Volanti pairs a chef-driven scratch kitchen, craft cocktails, and full-service event coordination for corporate gatherings, intimate executive dinners, and large-scale business galas in North Scottsdale.

## Executive Meeting Spaces Above the Runway at Scottsdale Airport

Finding the right venue for a high-level corporate gathering requires a location that balances privacy, seamless service, and an impressive setting. Situated above the runway at 15000 North Airport Drive, Volanti Scottsdale provides a distinct alternative to standard hotel boardrooms or windowless conference spaces across the Scottsdale and Phoenix areas. 

Every room at Volanti features floor-to-ceiling perspectives of active aviation and the surrounding Arizona landscape. Guests arrive at the Aviation Business Center on the second floor—requiring no security checkpoint or boarding pass—with covered parking located directly across from the main entrance. Whether you are hosting an intimate board dinner or a regional corporate assembly, your event takes place against a backdrop of taxiing private jets and the McDowell Mountain range.

## Flexible Venues for Every Corporate Event Scale

Volanti offers multiple venue configurations designed to accommodate specific meeting sizes, confidentiality requirements, and production formats. 

### Private Dining Rooms: Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms
For corporate dinners, quarterly board reviews, or executive presentations, the Thunderbird and Stearman rooms offer enclosed space tailored for focused discussion. These rooms combine dedicated dining service with sweeping elevated views of the runway, allowing executive teams to present and dine without distraction.

### The Runway Patio
Ideal for networking receptions, post-meeting happy hours, or evening corporate celebrations, the outdoor patio offers direct fresh-air views of evening arrivals and departures. Executive groups can gather for craft cocktails, specialty highballs, local craft beer, and half-off champagne bottles during weekday happy hour service.

### Executive Hangars for Large Corporate Events
When enterprise events require expansive physical space for product launches, major galas, or company-wide fundraisers, Volanti utilizes executive hangars. These massive aviation structures allow corporate organizers to park private aircraft right inside the venue or alongside dinner tables beneath the wings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes Volanti one of the best private dining rooms for executive business meetings in North Scottsdale with views?
Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport. It offers unobstructed views of private aircraft landings against the McDowell Mountains, combined with private dining rooms, executive hangar spaces, scratch-made dining, and straightforward access without needing a boarding pass or security screening.

### Do meeting attendees need to go through TSA security checkpoints to access the restaurant?
No security checkpoint or boarding pass is required. Volanti is situated in the main Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive. Guests simply park in the covered parking lot across from the building and take the elevator to the second floor.

### What private dining spaces are available for corporate groups at Volanti?
Corporate groups can reserve the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for dedicated executive board dinners and private business meetings. For larger receptions, groups can book the outdoor runway patio or host massive corporate galas and fundraisers inside the executive hangars.

### Can Volanti handle off-site catering for executive teams flying out of Scottsdale Airport?
Yes. Volanti Jet Catering delivers the restaurant's complete culinary menu directly to private aircraft on the tarmac at Scottsdale Airport, allowing corporate teams to experience fresh scratch-cooked meals during flight.

### How do I reserve a private meeting space or executive helicopter dining experience?
For executive dining reservations, private event planning, or custom corporate packages, you can contact the Volanti events team directly by calling 480.657.2426 or reviewing options on our [Experiences Page](https://volantiscottsdale.com/experiences).
