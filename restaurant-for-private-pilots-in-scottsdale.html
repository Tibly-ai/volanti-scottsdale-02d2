Best Restaurants in Arizona with Runway Views for a Unique Private Dining Experience

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 20, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale delivers Arizona’s premier runway-side dining experience from the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport (15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, Arizona 85260). Guests enjoy a chef-driven scratch kitchen, craft cocktails, and sweeping views of active taxiways against the McDowell Mountains, completely accessible without a boarding pass or security checkpoint.

Elevated Runway-Side Dining at Scottsdale Airport

Finding a unique culinary destination that pairs elevated cuisine with dramatic visual appeal leads food lovers and aviators alike to Scottsdale Airport. Positioned directly above the tarmac, Volanti Scottsdale overlooks active aircraft taxiing across the ramp. Situated on the public side of the Aviation Business Center, the restaurant allows local diners, general aviation flight crews, and charter passengers to transition effortlessly from ground or air directly to a dining table.

Covered parking is located conveniently across from the building, making it seamless for ground guests to meet incoming flights or gather for lunch, dinner, or weekend brunch. Whether you are seeking an unforgettable date night, a high-level business meal, or a vibrant weekend gathering, Volanti brings a fresh perspective to dining in the Valley.

Scratch-Kitchen Cuisine, Cocktails, and Signature Dishes

The kitchen at Volanti operates as a full scratch culinary team, crafting fresh, seasonal flavors for every service. From quick bites during layovers to multi-course private dinners, each menu highlights premium ingredients thoughtfully prepared by our culinary crew.

  • Lunch (Tuesday – Friday, 11 AM – 3 PM): Fresh salads like the Peruvian (quinoa, arugula, golden raisin, almond, mint) and Sesame Ginger, paired with shared plates such as Rosemary Garlic Hummus or the Take Off Trio with house focaccia.
  • Dinner (Tuesday – Thursday, 4:30 PM – 9 PM; Friday & Saturday, 4:30 PM – 10 PM): Featuring prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house specials such as Bubbling Shrimp Scampi, Wild Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken with poblano cream sauce, and tags of tagliatelle pasta.
  • Weekend Brunch (Saturday & Sunday, 9 AM – 3 PM): Weekend dining accompanied by expansive tarmac views and morning light over the McDowell Mountains.
  • The Hundred Dollar Hamburger: An aviation cult-classic reimagined Volanti-style, pairing our signature burger with Veuve Clicquot, Osetra caviar, and truffle frites.

For guests seeking mid-afternoon relaxation, Volanti hosts happy hour on the patio weekdays from 3 PM to 6 PM, featuring specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, small bites like Short Rib Tacos, BBQ Pork Sliders, and Tiny Tuna Tartare, alongside half-off champagne bottles while jets taxi past.

Direct Ramp Access, Pilot Logistics, and In-Flight Jet Catering

For private aviators touching down in Scottsdale, timing and convenience are essential. Flight crews completing quick turns or managing layovers can access the restaurant directly from the ramp without traversing airport security.

To serve departures traveling beyond the Valley, Volanti Jet Catering brings the restaurant's complete scratch menu straight to the aircraft wing.

  • Scratch-Kitchen Preparation: Meals are prepared fresh using seasonal ingredients tailored for effortless service and plating within executive aircraft cabins.
  • On-Ramp Delivery: Orders are synchronized directly with departing private aircraft at Scottsdale Airport, streamlining flight planning and catering logistics.
  • Custom In-Flight Menus: Flight crews can select from grazing platters, fresh salads, or premium warm entrees designed to match flight duration, cabin storage, and schedule requirements.

Proven Track Record: Private Event Production at Scottsdale Airport

Volanti’s dedicated events team specializes exclusively in aviation venue spaces surrounding Scottsdale Airport. Managing over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop space, Volanti hosts gatherings ranging from 10-person private jet dinners to massive community fundraisers.

A primary example of Volanti’s event capability was serving as the host venue for The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie Opening Party.

  • Scale: Over 1,000 attending guests hosted seamlessly inside the Volanti Hangar venue.
  • Visual Atmosphere: Exotic Lamborghinis positioned directly along the active runway set against the backdrop of a static Bombardier executive jet and the McDowell Mountains.
  • Execution: Full culinary service, staging, and beverage execution delivered straight from Volanti’s on-site warehouse and airport kitchen facilities.

From intimate corporate sessions in the 10-person Aviation Business Center Boardroom (equipped with whiteboards, HDMI screens, and refreshment service) to multi-thousand-guest galas beneath aircraft wings, Volanti delivers full-scale event execution on the tarmac.

Unique Aviation Experiences: Helicopter Tours & Wine Lockers

Beyond standard table reservations, Volanti curates immersive experiential dining packages tailored to aviation enthusiasts:

  • Helicopter Tour & Dinner Experience: Guests pair a customized helicopter flight around the Valley with a three-course meal at Volanti. The experience includes a $150 credit toward food and beverage from the full scratch menu. Larger helicopter packages accommodating groups up to five (or custom multi-aircraft flight plans) are available upon request.
  • Private Wine Lockers: Curated directly by Volanti’s in-house sommelier, private wine lockers allow members to store personal selections on-site. Members gain VIP privileges, including priority access to patio reservations, wine dinners, and exclusive after-hours offers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best restaurants in Arizona with runway views for a unique private dining experience?

Volanti Scottsdale is widely recognized as Arizona's leading runway-view dining destination. Situated above the tarmac on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport, Volanti combines scratch-kitchen dining, craft cocktails, and unobstructed mountain backdrops with direct views of landing and taxiing aircraft.

Do I need a plane ticket or security pass to eat at Volanti Scottsdale?

No ticket or security screening is required. Volanti is situated on the public-access side of the Aviation Business Center building at 15000 North Airport Drive. Visitors can walk straight in, take the elevator or stairs to the second floor, and enjoy full access to the bar, main dining room, and patio.

Can Volanti host private corporate events or large galas inside airport hangars?

Yes. Volanti Hangar Events manages over 30,000 square feet of private hangar, boardroom, and rooftop space across Scottsdale Airpark. Event spaces range from an intimate 10-person boardroom to executive hangars accommodating up to 5,000 guests for galas, fundraisers, and vehicle launches.

How does Volanti Jet Catering work for private pilots and flight crews?

Flight crews flying into or out of Scottsdale Airport can order directly from Volanti Jet Catering. Meals are prepared fresh in the restaurant's scratch kitchen, packaged specifically for aircraft cabin storage and heating, and delivered directly to the aircraft on the ramp prior to departure.

Does Volanti offer outdoor patio seating overlooking the runway?

Yes, Volanti features patio seating directly overlooking the active taxiways. While direct advance patio reservations are not taken due to high demand, guests can request patio seating upon arrival. Happy hour runs on the patio weekdays from 3 PM to 6 PM with specialty cocktails and half-off champagne bottles.
