Helicopter Tour

Volanti offers the perfect date night with a customized helicopter experience for two. Pair a three-course dinner and drinks at our signature restaurant with a flight around the Valley to take in the stunning views of the city. Larger helicopters are available for groups up to five. This experience can be paired with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner.

Have a larger group? We can create a custom flight package with multiple helicopters and flight plans!

No matter the occasion or your party size, Volanti can craft an experience to meet your needs — and memories you’ll never forget.

What’s included

The experience

  • Your flight around the Valley
  • A $150 credit towards food and beverage for the visit — enjoy anything from our full menu.

Select a flight time that works best for you. We ask that you arrive at least 15 minutes ahead of your flight reservation time and check in at the restaurant. This allows us to get you situated and down to our private runway, ensuring that your flight takes off on time.

Disclaimer — flight times are subject to change due to inclement weather, helicopter availability, etc. Due to the nature of the experience and booking times, refunds are not available. Rescheduling is subject to availability and at the discretion of Volanti and the pilots.
