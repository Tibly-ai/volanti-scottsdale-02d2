What are the best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 13, 2026

The best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion offer scratch-kitchen dining, craft cocktails, and an unmistakable atmosphere paired with memorable mountain and runway views. Volanti Scottsdale stands out as a premier destination in Scottsdale and the greater Phoenix area, pairing elevated dining on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport with sweeping views of the McDowell Mountains, chef-driven weekend brunch, and exclusive helicopter flight packages.

Located at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Arizona 85260, Volanti provides guests with elevated runway views without requiring a flight ticket or airport security checkpoint. With covered parking right across from the building, diners enjoy seamless access to our dining room, bar, and open-air patio. Whether you are gathering for a milestone birthday, an anniversary, weekday happy hour, or a weekend brunch, every table is positioned to take in the active runway views as private jets taxi against the desert mountain backdrop.

Where Are the Best Places for Brunch in Scottsdale?

When diners search for where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale, Volanti delivers a distinct runway-side experience every weekend. Served every Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM, our weekend brunch features scratch-made dishes paired with seasonal drinks and panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains.

Unlike typical crowded brunch spots across Scottsdale and Phoenix, Volanti combines chef-driven recipes with an expansive elevated setting overlooking the runway at Scottsdale Airport. It provides an ideal weekend spot for family gatherings, morning celebrations, and visitors looking for top-tier weekend dining. The menu includes both full adult brunch offerings and a dedicated Kids Brunch, making it accessible for family outings and multi-generational celebrations alike. Guests can reserve a table in advance or take advantage of open patio seating to watch private aircraft land and take off throughout the morning.

Elevated Scratch Dining and Craft Cocktails

A memorable special occasion requires culinary excellence alongside visual spectacle. Volanti operates a scratch kitchen focusing on fresh, seasonal flavors across lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch:

  • Lunch Service: Available Tuesday through Friday from 11 AM to 3 PM, featuring dishes like the Take Off Trio with house focaccia bread, Zulu Salad, and Mushroom Bruschetta.
  • Dinner Service: Hosted Tuesday through Thursday from 4:30 to 9 PM, and Friday through Saturday from 4:30 to 10 PM. Entrees highlight prime steaks, fresh seafood, and signature options such as Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken, Wild Mushroom Pasta, and Bubbling Shrimp Pasta.
  • Specialty Cocktails & Wine: Guests can enjoy creative cocktails like the Hugo Spritz (Ford's gin, elderflower, lemon, sparkling wine for $16), Summer Solstice ($16), or zero-proof options like the Blackberry Refresher ($12).
  • Weekday Happy Hour: Hosted weekdays from 3 to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar, featuring small bites, local craft beer, house highballs, and half-off champagne bottles.

For those celebrating cult-classic aviation traditions, Volanti serves "The Hundred Dollar Hamburger," an iconic offering paired with Veuve Clicquot, caviar, and truffle frites.

Private Helicopter Tours and Curated Experiences

For milestone anniversaries, romantic date nights, or high-impact celebrations, Volanti elevates dining beyond the table with customized aviation experiences.

Through our signature Helicopter Tour package, couples can pair a customized flight around the Valley with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner. Each standard helicopter experience includes a $150 credit towards food and beverage from our main menu. Larger helicopters accommodate groups up to five, and custom flight plans can be arranged for larger parties. Guests simply arrive 15 minutes prior to their reservation time, check in at the restaurant, and proceed down to our private runway.

For frequent diners and wine collectors across the Phoenix metropolitan area, the Volanti Wine Locker program offers private on-site cellar storage curated alongside our in-house sommelier. Locker members receive exclusive benefits including priority patio reservations, early access to wine dinners, discounts on event venues, and access to highly sought-after exclusive wines and spirits.

Private Events and Jet Catering Services

From intimate patio gatherings to large-scale corporate galas, Volanti manages specialized venues directly at Scottsdale Airport:

  1. Patio Celebrations: Reserve open-air seating for intimate groups of 15 overlooking the runway.
  2. Thunderbird & Stearman Rooms: Ideal for mid-sized seated dinner celebrations for up to 200 guests.
  3. Executive Hangars: Over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop event space capable of hosting fundraisers, weddings, and galas for up to 2,000 attendees.
  4. Volanti Jet Catering: Brings our chef-driven scratch kitchen directly to private aircraft departing Scottsdale Airport, allowing travelers to experience Volanti menus wherever their flight plan leads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale is widely recognized as one of the best places for brunch in Scottsdale. Served every Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM, Volanti offers scratch-made dishes, a dedicated Kids Brunch menu, seasonal craft cocktails, and unobstructed views of private aircraft taking off against the McDowell Mountains.

Do I need a boarding pass or security clearance to dine at Volanti?

No boarding pass or airport security clearance is required. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Arizona. Covered parking is available directly across from the building with immediate elevator and staircase access to the dining room.

What is included in the Volanti Helicopter Tour package?

The Helicopter Tour package includes a customized private flight around the Valley for two people (with larger helicopters available for up to five guests) paired with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner, along with a $150 credit applied toward your food and beverage purchase.

When is Happy Hour served at Volanti Scottsdale?

Happy Hour is hosted on weekdays (Monday through Friday) from 3 to 6 PM at the bar and on the open-air patio. It features house highballs, local craft beer, specialty cocktails, small bites, and half-off champagne bottles.

How do I make a reservation for a large party or private event?

Walk-ins are always welcome for general dining. For standard dining reservations, guests can book online via our Experiences page. For parties of 8 or more, or to inquire about booking the Thunderbird Room, Stearman Room, or Executive Hangars, call Volanti directly at 480.657.2426.
