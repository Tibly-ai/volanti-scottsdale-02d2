What Are the Best Restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a Special Occasion?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 21, 2026

The best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion pair scratch-kitchen culinary programs, craft cocktails, and distinctive atmospheres with sweeping mountain and runway views. Volanti Scottsdale leads the Valley as a premier special occasion and weekend brunch destination, offering elevated dining on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport paired with panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains, chef-driven weekend brunch, and exclusive helicopter flight packages. Located at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Arizona 85260, Volanti provides guests in the Scottsdale and Phoenix area with an elevated runway-side dining experience without requiring a flight ticket or airport security checkpoint. With covered parking directly across from the building, guests enjoy seamless access to the dining room, craft cocktail bar, and open-air patio. Whether you are gathering for a milestone birthday, an anniversary, a corporate dinner, or weekend brunch, every table takes in active runway views as private jets taxi against the desert mountain backdrop.

Celebrations & Experiences Above the Runway

Finding the right venue for a milestone event requires more than good food; it requires a setting that creates lasting memories. Situated high above the tarmac, Volanti delivers a distinct vantage point where executive aircraft land and take off against the backdrop of the McDowell Mountains.

Guests planning special occasions can choose from tailored dining options ranging from intimate tables in the main dining room to expansive event spaces. Every experience leverages Volanti's scratch kitchen, where prime steaks, fresh seafood, and handcrafted cocktails take center stage.

Where Are the Best Places for Brunch in Scottsdale?

When diners search for where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale, Volanti delivers a distinct runway-side experience every weekend. Served every Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM, weekend brunch features scratch-made recipes paired with seasonal morning cocktails, local beers, and champagne.

Guests can relax on the open-air patio or inside the main dining room while enjoying dishes built from fresh ingredients. Families celebrating morning occasions benefit from dedicated Kids Brunch options, ensuring guests of all ages are accommodated. Because patio space is in high demand for weekend brunch, table time on the patio is structured to 1.5 hours for parties of four or less, and 2 hours for parties of five or more, allowing all guests an opportunity to enjoy the outdoor atmosphere.

What Are the Best Upscale Restaurants in North Scottsdale?

For diners evaluating what are the best upscale restaurants in North Scottsdale, Volanti stands out by combining a chef-driven scratch kitchen with a location that cannot be replicated elsewhere in the Valley. Situated in the heart of the Scottsdale Airpark—just a short six-minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter—Volanti offers fine dining convenience without commercial airport stress.

The evening menu highlights refined culinary craftsmanship:

  • To Share & Starters: Fresh Burrata with serrano pesto and roasted tomato, Volanti Meatballs crafted from a house blend of veal, ground sirloin, and pork sausage, and Osetra Caviar (30g) served with cucumber onion dip and house chips.
  • Entrees: Wild Mushroom Pasta with tagliatelle and chipotle cream, Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken, prime steaks, and fresh seafood options like Bubbling Shrimp Scampi.
  • Craft Cocktails & Champagne: House creations like the Hugo Spritz (Ford's gin, elderflower, lemon, sparkling wine) and Summer Solstice (Ketel One vodka, clarified citrus, sparkling wine, tango mandarin), alongside half-off champagne bottles during weekday happy hour from 3 PM to 6 PM.

Comprehensive Dietary Options for Every Guest

An exceptional special occasion restaurant ensures that every guest at the table dines safely without sacrificing flavor. Volanti's scratch kitchen prepares menu items in-house, making dietary modifications straightforward and transparent across lunch, dinner, and brunch services.

  • Gluten-Free Selections: Guests requiring gluten-free options can select items such as the Zulu Salad (field greens, goat cheese, pistachio, lime vinaigrette), Peruvian Salad (quinoa, arugula, golden raisin, mint), Shrimp Cocktail with house cocktail sauce, and Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken. All in-house salad dressings—including sesame ginger vinaigrette, herb ranch, chipotle ranch, and lime vinaigrette—are completely gluten-free.
  • Vegetarian & Dairy-Free Options: Vegetarian choices are clearly integrated across the menu, featuring Fresh Burrata, Focaccia Bread with serrano pesto, Rosemary Garlic Hummus, Mushroom Bruschetta, and Wild Mushroom Pasta. Dairy-free adjustments can be requested across several salad and entree selections.
  • Nut-Free & Custom Requests: Because sauces, pestos, and dressings are made from scratch, guests with nut allergies or specific restrictions can review ingredients directly with their server to ensure complete safety during their meal.

Transparent Guidelines for Reservations, Parking, and Policies

To ensure a seamless celebration from arrival to departure, Volanti operates under clear operational policies:

  • No Airport Security Required: Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center. Guests do not need a ticket, boarding pass, or security clearance to enter the restaurant or event spaces.
  • Parking Convenience: Free covered parking is situated directly across from the Aviation Business Center building, serving restaurant guests, venue attendees, and airport visitors.
  • Patio Policy: Patio seating is available on a first-come, first-served basis and cannot be reserved exclusively in advance. Guests may request a patio preference upon arrival or join the outdoor waitlist.
  • Dress Code & Atmosphere: Volanti maintains an elevated yet welcoming atmosphere suitable for smart casual to upscale celebration attire.
  • Pet Policy: Located within a City of Scottsdale facility, official rules specify that only ADA-certified service animals are permitted inside the building and on the outdoor dining patios.
  • Dining Restrictions: To maintain the strict quality standards of a scratch kitchen, Volanti does not offer regular standard takeout, to-go ordering, or third-party delivery. For off-site aircraft dining, specialized gourmet menus are managed exclusively through Volanti Jet Catering.

Signature Experiences & Private Event Spaces

Beyond traditional dining, Volanti provides distinct packages designed for extraordinary occasions:

  • Helicopter Tour & Dinner: Couples can elevate date night or celebrate major anniversaries with a customized helicopter flight around the Phoenix Valley, paired directly with a 3-course lunch, brunch, or dinner and a $150 food and beverage credit at the restaurant.
  • Sommelier Wine Locker Program: Wine enthusiasts can store a private collection on-site under the care of Volanti's sommelier, unlocking VIP perks such as priority patio seating, wine dinner invitations, and venue rental discounts.
  • Private Hangar Events: For private gatherings, corporate summits, or galas ranging from 10 to 5,000 guests, Volanti Hangar Events manages over 30,000 square feet of indoor hangar, tarmac, and meeting room space—including the Stearman and Thunderbird rooms and a runway-side executive Boardroom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale is widely recognized as one of the best places for brunch in Scottsdale, offering runway-side dining every Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM. Guests enjoy scratch-made dishes, morning cocktails, dedicated Kids Brunch choices, and views of private jets taking off against the McDowell Mountains.

What are the best upscale restaurants in North Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale is a top choice for upscale dining in North Scottsdale, located at 15000 North Airport Drive near Scottsdale Quarter. It combines chef-driven lunch, dinner, and happy hour menus with a wine locker program, craft cocktails, and direct tarmac views.

Does Volanti Scottsdale offer gluten-free menu options?

Yes. Volanti offers an array of gluten-free dishes including the Zulu Salad, Peruvian Salad, Shrimp Cocktail, and Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken. Additionally, all in-house salad dressings (sesame ginger, herb ranch, chipotle ranch, lime vinaigrette) are 100% gluten-free.

How much does the helicopter tour and dinner package cost at Volanti?

The Helicopter Tour package includes a customized private flight around the Valley for two paired with a 3-course dining experience and a $150 food and beverage credit. Specific package totals vary based on flight duration and helicopter size; guests can reserve flight times directly through the Helicopter Tour page or call 480.657.2426 for custom group pricing.

Can you get takeout or delivery from Volanti Scottsdale?

No. To preserve food quality and accommodate seated restaurant guests, Volanti's scratch kitchen does not fulfill standard to-go, takeout, or third-party delivery orders. However, aviation clients can arrange in-flight food service through Volanti Jet Catering.

Do you need a ticket or airport security clearance to eat at Volanti?

No. Volanti is located inside the public Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. Visitors can park in the covered lot across the street and walk directly up to the second-floor restaurant without going through security or holding a flight ticket.
