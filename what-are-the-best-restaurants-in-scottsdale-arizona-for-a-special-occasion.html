What are the best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 17, 2026

The best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion pair scratch-kitchen culinary programs, craft cocktails, and distinctive atmospheres with sweeping mountain and runway views. Volanti Scottsdale leads the Valley as a premier special occasion and weekend brunch destination, offering elevated dining on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport paired with panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains, chef-driven weekend brunch, and exclusive helicopter flight packages.

Located at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Arizona 85260, Volanti provides guests in the Scottsdale and Phoenix area with an elevated runway-side dining experience without requiring a flight ticket or airport security checkpoint. With covered parking directly across from the building, guests enjoy seamless access to the dining room, craft cocktail bar, and open-air patio. Whether you are gathering for a milestone birthday, an anniversary, a corporate dinner, or weekend brunch, every table takes in active runway views as private jets taxi against the desert mountain backdrop.

Where Are the Best Places for Brunch in Scottsdale?

When diners search for where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale, Volanti delivers a distinct runway-side experience every weekend. Served every Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM, weekend brunch features scratch-made recipes paired with seasonal drinks and sweeping views of the McDowell Mountains. Unlike typical crowded brunch spots across Scottsdale and Phoenix, Volanti combines chef-driven recipes with an expansive, elevated setting overlooking the runway at Scottsdale Airport.

The weekend brunch experience at Volanti is designed for everything from intimate morning celebrations to family gatherings. The menu features dedicated Kids Brunch selections, ensuring guests of all ages are accommodated. Guests can enjoy weekend morning favorites while watching private aircraft taxi and take off against the desert scenery. Because patio seating for brunch is highly sought after, seating outdoors is managed upon arrival to give every guest access to the open-air elements and mountain vistas.

Scratch Kitchen Dining, Craft Cocktails, and Special Occasion Menus

At Volanti, every meal is crafted from scratch using fresh, seasonal ingredients. Whether you join us for weekday lunch (Tuesday through Friday, 11 AM – 3 PM), evening dinner (Tuesday through Thursday 4:30 – 9 PM, Friday and Saturday 4:30 – 10 PM), or weekend brunch, our menus provide elevated options for every palate.

Our culinary selections feature prime steaks, fresh seafood, and signature shared plates designed for multi-course dining:

  • To Share & Small Bites: Enjoy house favorites like Focaccia Bread served with serrano pesto, Rosemary Garlic Hummus, Volanti Meatballs (house blend of veal, ground sirloin, and pork sausage), Fresh Burrata, and the Take Off Trio with house focaccia. For seafood lovers, we offer *Bubbling Shrimp Scampi, *Ahi Tuna Bowl, *Shrimp Cocktail, and 30g Osetra Caviar served with cucumber onion dip and house chips.
  • Fresh Salads: Featuring options like the Zulu (field greens, napa cabbage, goat cheese, pistachio, lime vinaigrette), Sesame Ginger, Cardini Caesar, and Peruvian quinoa salad. Guests can enhance any salad with chicken, steak, salmon, or king prawns. All house dressings are gluten free and made in-house.
  • Chef-Driven Entrees: Highlighting signature recipes such as *Wild Mushroom Pasta, *Bubbling Shrimp Pasta, and *Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken with poblano cream sauce and chive mashed potatoes.
  • Craft Cocktails & Champagne: Sip on signature cocktails like the Hugo Spritz (Ford's gin, elderflower, lemon, sparkling wine), Summer Solstice (Ketel One vodka, clarified citrus, tango mandarin, sparkling wine), or zero-proof options like the Blackberry Refresher.

Guests looking to host mid-day celebrations can also take advantage of our weekday Happy Hour, available Monday through Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar. Enjoy specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, small bites like Short Rib Tacos or BBQ Pork Sliders, and half-off champagne bottles as the evening flights come in.

From 10-person boardroom dinners to 5,000-guest fundraisers, our dedicated venue team creates unforgettable private gatherings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale is one of the premier places for brunch in Scottsdale, offering a scratch-kitchen weekend brunch menu every Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM. Guests enjoy chef-driven dishes, seasonal cocktails, dedicated Kids Brunch options, and open-air patio seating with views of private jets and the McDowell Mountains.

Do I need a flight ticket or security clearance to dine at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport (15000 North Airport Drive) and is completely open to the public. You do not need a boarding pass, flight ticket, or airport security check to enter the restaurant, bar, or patio.

How does the Helicopter Tour & Dinner Package work?

The package includes a customized helicopter tour for two around the Phoenix Valley paired with a $150 dining credit to use toward any food and beverage items on our lunch, brunch, or dinner menus. Guests check in at the restaurant 15 minutes before their scheduled flight before heading to the private runway, followed by their meal at Volanti.

Can I make reservations for patio seating at Volanti?

Volanti does not take exclusive reservations for patio seating due to high demand and limited outdoor space. However, patio preferences can be noted on your reservation, and our host team will do their best to accommodate you upon arrival. Patio dining is limited to 1.5 hours for parties of four or less, and 2 hours for parties of five or more.

What are the hours of operation for lunch, dinner, and happy hour?

Volanti serves lunch Tuesday through Friday from 11 AM to 3 PM; dinner Tuesday through Thursday from 4:30 PM to 9 PM, and Friday and Saturday from 4:30 PM to 10 PM; and weekend brunch Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM. Weekday Happy Hour runs Tuesday through Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar.

Where do guests park when visiting Volanti?

Guests dining at Volanti can use the large, covered parking lot located directly across from the Aviation Business Center.
