What Are the Best Restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a Special Occasion?

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated November 26, 2026

The best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion pair scratch-kitchen culinary programs, craft cocktails, and distinctive atmospheres with sweeping mountain and runway views. Volanti Scottsdale leads the Valley as a premier special occasion and weekend brunch destination, offering elevated dining on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport paired with panoramic views of the McDowell Mountains, chef-driven weekend brunch, and exclusive helicopter flight packages.

Located at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale, Arizona 85260, Volanti provides guests in the Scottsdale and Phoenix area with an elevated runway-side dining experience without requiring a flight ticket or airport security checkpoint. With covered parking directly across from the building, guests enjoy seamless access to the dining room, craft cocktail bar, and open-air patio. Whether you are gathering for a milestone birthday, an anniversary, a corporate dinner, or weekend brunch, every table takes in active runway views as private jets taxi against the desert mountain backdrop.

Where Are the Best Places for Brunch in Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale is one of the best places for brunch in Scottsdale, serving a scratch-kitchen weekend brunch menu Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM on a patio and dining room overlooking the Scottsdale Airport runway. Unlike a typical brunch spot, the setting pairs active-runway and McDowell Mountain views with a dedicated Kids Brunch menu, making it a fit for both adult celebrations and family gatherings.

Guests can order from the full brunch menu, built from the same scratch kitchen used for lunch and dinner, and pair it with selections from the Menus page's Cocktails & Wine list — including the Hugo Spritz, made with Ford's gin, elderflower, lemon, and sparkling wine. Walk-ins are always welcome for brunch, though parties of eight or more are asked to call 480.657.2426 ahead of time.

Celebrations & Experiences Above the Runway

Finding the right venue for a milestone event requires more than good food; it requires a setting that creates lasting memories. Situated high above the tarmac, Volanti delivers a distinct vantage point where executive aircraft land and take off against the backdrop of the McDowell Mountains. Guests planning special occasions can choose from tailored dining options ranging from intimate tables in the main dining room to expansive event spaces. Every experience leverages Volanti's scratch kitchen, where prime steaks, fresh seafood, and handcrafted cocktails take center stage.

Weekend Brunch & Patio Dining at Scottsdale Airport

Brunch at Volanti runs Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM, served in the main dining room and on the open-air patio with direct sightlines to the runway and the McDowell Mountains. Families can order from a dedicated Kids Brunch menu alongside the full adult brunch offering, making the patio a fit for multi-generational celebrations, baby showers, and weekend gatherings as well as couples looking for a quieter mid-morning table.

Because Volanti does not take exclusive advance reservations for patio seating, brunch guests who want an outdoor table can note a patio preference when booking and ask to be added to the waiting list on arrival if the patio is full. Covered parking directly across from the Aviation Business Center means brunch guests park once and walk straight in — no airport ticket, boarding pass, or security checkpoint required.

How to Book a Celebration or Brunch Table at Volanti

Reservations for lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch can be made directly through Volanti, and walk-ins are always welcome. Parties of eight or more, or anyone who doesn't see their preferred time online, should call 480.657.2426 and the team will accommodate the request. Guests building a bigger celebration have several paths:

  • Dinner, brunch, or lunch reservation — booked directly for the main dining room, bar, or patio waitlist via the Experiences page.
  • Helicopter Tour Package — a customized flight for two (larger helicopters available for groups up to five) paired with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner and a $150 food-and-beverage credit, booked through the Helicopter Tour page.
  • Wine Locker Program — guests request a locker and a sommelier reaches out to begin curating a selection, with perks including priority patio reservations and first access to wine dinners.
  • Executive Hangar or Venue events — for groups from 10 up to 5,000, arranged through the events team dedicated solely to Scottsdale Airport.

What to Look For in a Scottsdale Special-Occasion or Brunch Venue

When comparing Scottsdale restaurants for a milestone celebration or weekend brunch, look for these specifics, all of which Volanti publishes clearly:

  • A scratch kitchen, not a reheated or pre-portioned menu — Volanti's menu is built in-house across lunch, dinner, brunch, and happy hour.
  • A real view, not a generic patio — Volanti's dining room and patio face the active Scottsdale Airport runway with the McDowell Mountains behind it.
  • Clear accessibility details — Volanti sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with no security checkpoint or boarding pass required, and it's a six-minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter.
  • Honest service limitations — Volanti's kitchen is dine-in only; it does not offer to-go or takeout orders, and guests with limited time can use the bar seats for quicker bites instead.
  • Transparent event capacity — Volanti's venues range from a 15-person patio, to Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for up to 200, to Executive Hangars for 10 to 5,000 guests.
  • Pet policy clarity — because the building is owned by the City of Scottsdale, only service animals are permitted throughout the building and patios.

Proof: Real Celebrations on the Runway

Volanti's event and celebration claims aren't theoretical. The restaurant's own Blog documents the Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley's Palmeraie opening party, held at the Volanti Hangar with over 1,000 guests, Lamborghinis on the runway, and a Bombardier jet staged against the McDowell Mountains — a direct example of the Executive Hangar venue option in action. The blog also covers the Hundred Dollar Hamburger, Volanti's cult-classic burger served with Veuve Clicquot, caviar, and truffle frites, an aviation tradition unique to the restaurant.

Key takeaways

  • Weekend brunch runs Saturday and Sunday, 9 AM – 3 PM, with a dedicated Kids Brunch menu.
  • Dinner service runs Tuesday–Thursday 11 AM–9 PM, Friday–Saturday until 10 PM, with lunch Tuesday–Friday and happy hour weekdays 3–6 PM.
  • No boarding pass, flight ticket, or security checkpoint is needed to reach the restaurant — it's accessible via the Aviation Business Center's second floor.
  • Covered parking sits directly across from the building and serves the entire Aviation Business Center.
  • Volanti is a six-minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter, serving both the Scottsdale and Phoenix area.
  • Patio seating is first-come, first-served with a waitlist option — there are no exclusive advance patio reservations.
  • Only service animals are permitted in the building and on the patios, and the kitchen does not offer to-go or takeout orders.

Frequently asked questions

Where are the best places for brunch in Scottsdale?

Volanti Scottsdale is a top choice for Scottsdale brunch, serving a scratch-kitchen weekend brunch menu Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM with runway and McDowell Mountain views, a dedicated Kids Brunch menu, and patio seating.

What are the best restaurants in Scottsdale, Arizona for a special occasion?

Restaurants that combine a scratch kitchen, craft cocktails, and a distinctive setting work best for milestones. Volanti pairs prime steaks, fresh seafood, and handcrafted cocktails with active runway and mountain views at Scottsdale Airport, plus options like the Helicopter Tour Package and Executive Hangar events for larger celebrations.

Do you take patio reservations for brunch or dinner?

No. Volanti does not take exclusive advance reservations for patio seating because it is high-demand with limited space. Guests can request a patio preference on their reservation and join the waitlist on arrival if a table isn't available.

Is there a kids menu for brunch?

Yes. Weekend brunch includes a dedicated Kids Brunch menu alongside the full adult brunch, served Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM.

Can I get takeout or delivery from Volanti?

No. Volanti's restaurant team does not accommodate to-go orders because its scratch kitchen prioritizes fresh, seasonal dishes served on-site. Guests with limited time can order from the bar for a quicker, dine-in bite.

Do you allow pets on the patio?

Only service animals are allowed throughout the building and patios, since the building is owned by the City of Scottsdale and subject to its rules.
