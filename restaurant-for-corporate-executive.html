Restaurant for Corporate Executive in Scottsdale

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 26, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale delivers runway-side dining, executive hosting, and private event venues directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport. Positioned on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with sweeping views of the McDowell Mountains, Volanti offers high-level executives a seamless location for client lunches, strategic dinners, weekday happy hours, and large-scale corporate events.

Executive Dining and Meeting Constraints at Scottsdale Airport

Corporate leaders operating in Scottsdale and the broader Phoenix area face distinct demands when coordinating business meals and executive gatherings. Precision timing, privacy, high-quality scratch cooking, and convenient logistics are essential when meeting with board members, prospective partners, or out-of-town stakeholders.

Volanti Scottsdale addresses these executive priorities by combining an elevated culinary experience with an unmistakable airport backdrop. Situated at 15000 North Airport Drive, the restaurant eliminates typical travel delays for visiting aviation executives and local corporate teams alike. Guests arrive at the Aviation Business Center, access covered parking directly across from the building, and head straight to the second floor. No boarding pass or security checkpoint is required to access the dining room, patio, or private spaces.

Whether hosting an intimate four-person strategic lunch during midday service or coordinating an evening celebration for key stakeholders, executives benefit from an atmosphere designed around speed, professionalism, and panoramic views of taxiing jets and mountain peaks.

Executive Lunch and Strategic Dinners

For daytime meetings, Volanti operates lunch service Tuesday through Friday from 11 AM to 3 PM. Executive teams can conduct business over house-made focaccia, fresh salads, and scratch entrees while maintaining a structured schedule. Dinner service runs Tuesday through Thursday from 4:30 PM to 9 PM, and Friday through Saturday from 4:30 PM to 10 PM, offering prime steaks, fresh seafood, and craft cocktails framed by the evening runway lights.

Weekday Happy Hour Hosting

Executives holding informal team debriefs or client networking sessions can utilize happy hour on the patio, running weekdays from 3 PM to 6 PM. The menu features specialty cocktails, house highballs, local craft beer, select wine, and half-off champagne bottles alongside shared small plates like Volanti Meatballs, Short Rib Tacos, and Tiny Tuna Tartare.

Hangar Events and Large-Scale Buyouts

When corporate hosting extends beyond the main dining room, Volanti’s dedicated events team manages over 30,000 square feet of hangar, runway, and rooftop event space. Company leadership can host dinner celebrations for 200 guests in the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms or scale up to a gala or fundraiser for up to 2,000 guests inside the executive hangars.

Direct Aircraft Catering

For executives traveling via private aviation, Volanti Jet Catering brings the restaurant’s full scratch menu directly onto the flight line, ensuring culinary consistency from the runway to the sky.

What Corporate Executives Should Look for in an Airport Venue

When selecting a corporate dining partner near Scottsdale Airport, executives should evaluate key operational factors to ensure meetings run without friction:

  1. Frictionless Logistics: Venue accessibility is critical. Volanti features covered parking directly across from the Aviation Business Center and direct elevator access to the second floor, bypassing airport security entirely.
  2. Dedicated Event Management: Large corporate functions require specialized oversight. Volanti maintains an events team dedicated solely to the Scottsdale Airport site to manage layout, service timing, and venue execution.
  3. Menu Versatility: Executive groups often present varying schedules and dining preferences. A chef-driven scratch kitchen capable of serving quick midday lunches, multi-course dinners, and high-volume event catering ensures all dietary and itinerary requirements are met.
  4. Signature Hospitality Experiences: Differentiating a corporate dinner often comes down to unique amenities. Options like Volanti’s Wine Lockers program—which offers private cellar storage curated with an in-house sommelier—or custom multi-course flight experiences via the Helicopter Tour package offer memorable touchpoints for executive VIPs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reserve a table or private room for a corporate team?

Individual tables for lunch, dinner, or brunch can be booked directly online through our Experiences and Reservations page. For parties of 8 or more, or to discuss private dining rooms, corporate executives are encouraged to call our team at 480.657.2426.

Where do executive guests park when visiting Volanti Scottsdale?

Guests park in the covered parking structure located directly across from the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive. From there, head straight to the second floor.

Can Volanti cater directly to private jets on the runway?

Yes. Volanti Jet Catering delivers the restaurant’s chef-driven scratch kitchen menu straight to your aircraft at Scottsdale Airport.

What event venues are available for large corporate galas?

Volanti offers over 30,000 square feet of venue space, including the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms for up to 200 guests and executive hangars capable of hosting up to 2,000 guests beneath the wings of private aircraft.

Is a boarding pass or security clearance required to dine at Volanti?

No. Volanti is an open-to-the-public restaurant located inside the Aviation Business Center. No security checkpoints or flight tickets are required to enter.
