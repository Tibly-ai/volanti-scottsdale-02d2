Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you take to-go orders?

Our restaurant team is unable to accommodate to-go orders.

Volanti operates a scratch kitchen that prioritizes fresh, seasonal offerings. In consideration of these constraints and the need to accommodate onsite guests, we do not offer any to-go options. For guests with limited time, bar seats are available for quick bites.

Do you have to be flying out of the airport or go through security to get to the restaurant?

No. We are conveniently located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center overlooking Scottsdale Airport. Although it is a full-service airport, the Business Center has direct access to the restaurant without you having to be flying out or pass through any security checkpoints.

Where do you park?

We have a large, covered parking lot across from our building. This area can be used by the entire Aviation Business Center, including Volanti Restaurant, the City of Scottsdale Airport staff, our venue, and United States Customs.

Do you take patio reservations?

We do not take exclusive reservations for our patio seating. As the patio is a high-demand area with very limited space, we cannot guarantee availability in advance. To accommodate all of our guests’ seating preferences, patio seating is limited to an hour and a half for parties of four or less, and two hours for parties of five or more.

We can add a preference for patio seating to your reservation and will do our best to accommodate that request when you arrive. If patio seating is not available at your reservation time, you may let the hostess know that you would prefer to go on the waiting list for a seat outdoors.

Can you walk from Kierland or Scottsdale Quarter?

Yes — we’re just a short six-minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter!

Do you allow pets?

We are located inside a building owned by the City of Scottsdale. Per City rules and regulations, only service animals are allowed throughout the building and patios.

Can we book flights with you?

Yes! Though we don’t charter any of our own planes, we have partners that can take you anywhere you want to go. From our Helicopter Experience partners to our hangar owners, we can find a way to get you in the air!
