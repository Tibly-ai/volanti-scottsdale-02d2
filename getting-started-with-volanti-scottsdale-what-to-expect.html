Getting Started With Volanti Scottsdale: What to Expect

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 27, 2026

slug: getting-started-with-restaurant
meta_title: Getting Started at Volanti Scottsdale | Runway-Side Dining
meta_description: Everything you need to know for your first visit to Volanti Scottsdale. Learn about parking, reservations, dress code, and runway-side dining options.

Volanti Scottsdale offers a chef-driven, scratch-kitchen dining experience situated directly above the runway on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at Scottsdale Airport. Visitors can enjoy lunch, dinner, weekend brunch, and weekday happy hour with unobstructed views of jet takeoffs and the McDowell Mountains—with no boarding pass, security checkpoint, or flight ticket required.

Is Volanti Scottsdale the Right Dining Experience for You?

Volanti Scottsdale is designed for guests looking for a unique upscale dining environment in Scottsdale, Arizona. Located at 15000 North Airport Drive, the restaurant combines fresh seasonal dishes, signature craft cocktails, and an active aviation background.

Volanti caters to a wide variety of dining occasions:

  • Casual Weekday Lunch & Happy Hour: Ideal for business lunches, mid-day breaks, or weekday afternoon drinks on the patio while watching jets taxi past.
  • Evening Dining & Cocktails: Perfect for dinner dates, anniversary celebrations, or small group dinners paired with fine wine and mountain views.
  • Weekend Brunch: Designed for relaxed Saturday and Sunday morning gatherings overlooking the airfield.
  • Specialty Experiences & Private Events: Geared toward guests seeking extraordinary outings, including private flight packages, wine storage programs, and corporate or social event hosting.

Whether you are local to the Phoenix Metro area or visiting Scottsdale for the first time, Volanti offers accessible runway-side seating without the hassle of airport terminal security.

What to Decide Before You Book or Visit

To ensure a seamless experience on your first visit to Volanti Scottsdale, keep the following choices in mind:

1. Select Your Dining Occasion and Time

Volanti operates under distinct service hours throughout the week:

  • Lunch: Tuesday through Friday from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
  • Dinner: Tuesday through Thursday from 4:30 PM to 9:00 PM; Friday and Saturday from 4:30 PM to 10:00 PM.
  • Brunch: Saturday and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
  • Happy Hour: Weekdays from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

2. Choose Your Seating Setting

Guests can choose between indoor dining room seating, bar top seating, or the outdoor patio overlooking the runway. Patio seating provides direct views of the aviation activity and sunset over the McDowell Mountains, making it a popular choice for weekday happy hour.

3. Consider Specialized Experiences

If you are celebrating a landmark occasion, decide if a standard dining table fits your needs or if you want to elevate the visit:

  • Helicopter Tour Experience: Combine a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner with a private flight over the Valley.
  • Private Wine Lockers: Store a personalized, sommelier-curated collection on-site for exclusive access during every visit.

What to Expect Upon Arrival at Scottsdale Airport

Finding and entering Volanti Scottsdale is straightforward when you know where to go:

  • Location: We are located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, AZ 85260.
  • Parking: Covered parking is conveniently located right across from the main Aviation Business Center building.
  • Entering the Restaurant: Enter the main lobby of the Aviation Business Center and take the elevator or stairs to the second floor. No boarding pass, TSA screening, or security checkpoint is required to enter the restaurant.
  • Check-In: Walk-ins are always welcome. If you hold a reservation or are checked in for a flight experience, speak directly to the host hoststand upon arriving on the second floor.

Navigating the Volanti Menu

Volanti operates a scratch kitchen focusing on prime steaks, fresh seafood, shared plates, and custom cocktails. When dining with us, you can explore several menu highlights:

  • Shared Plates & Starters: Items like the Take Off Trio with house focaccia, Volanti Meatballs, Fresh Burrata, and Ahi Tuna Bowl.
  • Craft Cocktails & Wine: Signature drinks such as the Hugo Spritz and Summer Solstice, zero-proof options like the Blackberry Refresher, local craft beers, and half-off champagne bottles during weekday happy hour.
  • Dietary Modifications: All house-made salad dressings (including sesame ginger vinaigrette, herb ranch, chipotle ranch, and lime vinaigrette) are gluten-free. Vegan and vegetarian items (such as the Mushroom Bruschetta and Fresh Burrata) are labeled on the menu.

Planning Large Group Dining and Private Events in Scottsdale

If you are planning a group outing, dinner party, or corporate event in the Scottsdale area, Volanti offers tailored venue options depending on your party size:

  1. Parties Under 8 Guests: Reservations can be made through our standard booking channels or by walking in.
  2. Parties of 8 or More: Call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426 so our staff can seat and accommodate your group together.
  3. Private Events (15 to 2,000+ Guests): Volanti manages dedicated venue spaces at the airport. You can host a birthday gathering for 15 on the patio, a dinner for 200 in the Thunderbird & Stearman rooms, or large-scale galas for up to 2,000 guests in our executive hangars.

To explore options for private hostings, review our private event and venue services.

Key Takeaways for First-Time Guests

  • Location: Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center (15000 N Airport Drive, Scottsdale, AZ) with covered parking across the street.
  • No Airport Security Needed: You do not need a flight ticket, security pass, or boarding pass to enter and dine.
  • Reservations & Walk-Ins: Walk-ins are welcome; parties of 8 or more should call 480.657.2426 in advance.
  • Happy Hour: Enjoy weekday happy hour from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM on the patio with drink specials and half-off champagne bottles.
  • Specialty Experiences: Private flight packages and curated wine locker memberships are available alongside daily restaurant dining.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a boarding pass or ticket to dine at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti is an open-to-the-public restaurant located inside the Aviation Business Center. You do not pass through airport security checkpoints or TSA screening to reach our dining room or patio.

How do I make a reservation for a large party?

For groups of 8 or more guests, please call us directly at 480.657.2426 so our team can coordinate seating arrangements and menu options for your party.

Where do I park when visiting the restaurant?

Covered parking is available right across from the Aviation Business Center building at 15000 North Airport Drive.

What experiences does Volanti offer besides regular dining?

Volanti offers several signature experiences, including the Helicopter Tour and dinner package, private Wine Lockers program, jet catering for private aircraft, and large-scale hangar event venues.

What are Volanti's regular hours of operation?

We are open Tuesday through Thursday from 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Friday and Saturday from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM (with Saturday brunch starting at 9:00 AM), and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Happy hour runs Monday through Friday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
