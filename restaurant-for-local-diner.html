Restaurant for Local Diners in Scottsdale

By Volanti Scottsdale · Updated October 24, 2026

Volanti Scottsdale offers scratch-kitchen dining, craft cocktails, and mountain views directly above the runway at Scottsdale Airport, located at 15000 North Airport Drive, Scottsdale, Arizona 85260. Local diners can join us on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center for lunch, dinner, weekend brunch, and weekday happy hour, with covered parking right across from the building and no security checkpoint required.

Scratch-Kitchen Dining and Runway Views in Scottsdale

For local diners seeking a distinct dining destination in Scottsdale, Volanti Scottsdale provides a complete culinary experience set against the backdrop of active aircraft takeoffs and landings. Operating from the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, our establishment pairs chef-driven cuisine with sweeping views of the runway and the McDowell Mountains.

Local guests visiting for midday meals, evening gatherings, or weekend outings experience a scratch kitchen focused on fresh, seasonal flavors. Unlike typical airport facilities, our dining space is entirely open to the public. You do not need a boarding pass, flight reservation, or security clearance to visit. Covered parking is positioned directly across from the Aviation Business Center, allowing seamless access for residents across Scottsdale and the greater Phoenix area.

Whether you are stopping in for a quick lunch during the workweek or gathering with friends for an extended weekend meal, our dining room and outdoor patio space are designed so that every table has a view of the tarmac and surrounding desert landscape.

Dining Options: Lunch, Dinner, Weekend Brunch, and Happy Hour

Local diners have access to tailored menus across different times of the day, ensuring a fit for casual daytime meals, late-afternoon drinks, or formal evening dining.

  • Lunch (Tuesday – Friday, 11 AM – 3 PM): Midday guests can choose from fresh salads, shared plates, and scratch-made specialties. Offerings include the Zulu salad with field greens, napa cabbage, goat cheese, and lime vinaigrette, or shared options like the Take Off Trio served with house focaccia.
  • Dinner (Tuesday – Thursday, 4:30 – 9 PM; Friday & Saturday, 4:30 – 10 PM): Evening dining highlights prime steaks, fresh seafood, and house signature entrees. Guests can enjoy items such as Wild Mushroom Pasta, Mediterranean Stuffed Chicken with poblano cream sauce, or Bubbling Shrimp Scampi alongside our full cocktail and wine list.
  • Weekend Brunch (Saturday & Sunday, 9 AM – 3 PM): Brunch visitors can enjoy dedicated morning items, specialized kids brunch selections, and full beverage service overlooking the morning runway traffic.
  • Weekday Happy Hour (Monday – Friday, 3 – 6 PM): Held at the bar and out on the patio, happy hour features house highballs, specialty cocktails, local craft beer, select red and white wines, and half-off all champagne bottles. Guests can pair drinks with light bites, including Short Rib Tacos, Volanti Meatballs, Tiny Tuna Tartare, and BBQ Pork Sliders.

What Local Diners Should Look for in an Airport Runway Dining Experience

When evaluating runway dining options in the Phoenix metropolitan area, local residents should consider logistical ease, atmosphere, and kitchen quality:

  1. Public Accessibility: Confirm that the restaurant does not require security screening or airport badging. Volanti is fully accessible to the public on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center.
  2. Parking and Arrival: Convenience matters for local diners. Volanti offers covered parking directly across from the entrance, eliminating long walks or complicated airport parking structures.
  3. Kitchen Standards: Seek out scratch-made menus rather than standard bar fare. Volanti operates a chef-driven kitchen utilizing fresh ingredients, house-made dressings, and freshly prepared sauces.
  4. Seating and Views: Ensure the layout maximizes the location. Volanti is situated to give guests in the main dining room, bar, and patio clear views of the runway and McDowell Mountains.
  5. Varied Dining Formats: Look for venues that accommodate different dining occasions, from quick patio happy hours to multi-course special occasion dinners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a ticket or boarding pass to eat at Volanti Scottsdale?

No. Volanti is located on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center and is fully open to the public. You do not need a boarding pass, flight ticket, or airport security check to enter and dine.

Where do local diners park when visiting Volanti?

Covered parking is conveniently located directly across from the Aviation Business Center at 15000 North Airport Drive in Scottsdale.

What are the hours for happy hour at Volanti?

Happy hour runs weekdays (Monday through Friday) from 3 PM to 6 PM on the patio and at the bar. It features specialty cocktails, local craft beer, house highballs, small bites, and half-off all champagne bottles.

How do I make a reservation for a large group?

For parties of eight or more guests, please call the restaurant directly at 480.657.2426 so our staff can arrange proper seating. Smaller parties can reserve tables online or walk in.

What is included in the Helicopter Tour dining package?

The helicopter package includes a customized flight around the Valley for two (or up to five in larger aircraft) paired with a $150 credit towards food and beverage at Volanti for lunch, brunch, or dinner.
